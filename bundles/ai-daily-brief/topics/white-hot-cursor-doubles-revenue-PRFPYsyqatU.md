# Overview

In this headlines edition, Whittemore leads with Bloomberg's report that Cursor surpassed $2 billion in ARR in February — doubling in three months — a "massive shock" to power users on X who had written Cursor off after hopping between Claude Code and Codex. He frames the story as proof that AI coding startups are not playing the zero-sum game the "chattering class" imagines: enterprise diffusion is glacial compared to startup tool-switching, and the whole segment is growing fast.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]

The rest of the episode covers a Claude outage driven by "unprecedented demand" following the DOD kerfuffle that sent Anthropic downloads soaring and ChatGPT uninstalls spiking, Sam Altman's memo revising OpenAI's Pentagon contract to bar domestic surveillance uses, Washington's scramble over AI policy after agencies dropped Claude, and speculation that a metallic puck spotted with Joe Gebbia is the Jony Ive OpenAI device from a supposedly hoaxed Super Bowl ad.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]

# Key points

- Sources told Bloomberg that Cursor passed $2B ARR in February, doubling from $1B in three months, with 60% of revenue from corporate customers via new sign-ups and seat expansion.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]
- Kyle Russell of Valon described a cascade of 90 canceled Cursor seats after his CEO went "so deep into Claude Code"; Deedy Das of Menlo Ventures called the revenue numbers a "narrative violation," noting Claude Code went from 0 to $2.5B in 8 months while enterprise adoption of Cursor is only beginning.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]
- Investor Hubert Thieblot: "The adoption S curve still has tons of runway left. Early adopters might be moving on, but the mainstream is finally showing up"; Job van der Voort of Remote noted Cursor is "amazing for large code bases shared across many engineers."[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]
- Claude suffered a Monday-morning outage peaking around 6:40 a.m.; Anthropic cited "unprecedented demand for Claude over the last week" while consumer services stayed down.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]
- Per Sensor Tower, ChatGPT uninstalls tripled in a day after the DOD kerfuffle, US daily downloads fell 13% then another 5%, and one-star reviews surged 775% on Saturday; the Claude app gained 37% then 51% in downloads, and Similarweb showed Claude downloads up roughly 20x in a month, driving it to number one in the App Store.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]
- Sam Altman told staff the OpenAI Pentagon deal "looked a little sloppy" and will add language barring intentional use for domestic surveillance of US persons, with the DOD agreeing OpenAI systems won't be used by intelligence agencies like the NSA; Altman also said Anthropic should not be designated a supply chain risk and should get the same terms.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's core takeaway is that the Cursor numbers "hammer home that AI startups just aren't playing a zero-sum game": the current dynamic is not Claude Code taking share from Cursor but the entire agentic-coding segment growing rapidly and sustainably — as one commenter put it, "AI coding agents aren't hype anymore. They're infrastructure." On the political front he notes AI policy has been thrust on Washington as a live, high-stakes issue that is scrambling normal partisan lines, and he remains watchful on whether the consumer switching from ChatGPT to Claude persists beyond the active boycott moment.[^white-hot-cursor-doubles-revenue-PRFPYsyqatU]

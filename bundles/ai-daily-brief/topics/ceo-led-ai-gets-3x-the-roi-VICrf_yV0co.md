# Overview

The main episode digs into KPMG's Quarterly Pulse Survey — valuable, Nathaniel Whittemore argues, because it is longitudinal and was collected in the true agentic era rather than "the before times." The headline findings: senior leaders saying AI is driving meaningful business value jumped from 64% to 76%; organizations are shifting from efficiency priorities (productivity, cost reduction, all declining) toward strategic "opportunity AI" priorities (human-AI collaboration, governance, ecosystems, all rising); and accountability is decisive — organizations with clear AI accountability were 3x more likely to report ROI, and where the CEO is accountable, 14% report established ROI versus 4% where the CEO is not, with meaningful-business-value reports at 57% versus 21%.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]

An extended headlines section covers OpenAI's first in-house chip (Jalapeño, an inference ASIC built with Broadcom in a record 9-month design-to-tapeout cycle), prediction-market whiplash over a possible Fable 5 return as the White House sidelines Dario Amodei in favor of Tom Brown, vendor-lock-in criticism of Claude Tag, Anthropic's accusation that Alibaba ran "the largest distillation attack ever detected," continued DeepMind talent departures, and Micron's blowout earnings reversing a week of bubble jitters.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]

# Key points

- OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first custom chip, co-designed with Broadcom — an inference-focused ASIC like Google's TPUs, taken from design to tapeout in 9 months, which Greg Brockman credited partly to AI-enhanced design; Brockman reaffirmed OpenAI "cannot get compute fast enough," and Broadcom's Hock Tan said demand is "simply insatiable" through 2028.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Prediction-market odds of Fable 5 returning by July 1st jumped from 15% to 63% after Wired reported the Trump administration is "sick of" Dario Amodei but happy to negotiate with co-founder and chief compute officer Tom Brown ("not being a weirdo like Dario and can actually engage"); Claude Code snippets hinted at Fable 5 inclusion in subscriptions with weekly limits, and it reappeared on Amazon Bedrock — but no timeline exists, with talks focused on proof to alleviate jailbreak concerns.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Claude Tag drew backlash: Karpathy defended it as "an org-level harness," not "some crappy Slack bot," while critics warned that embedding organizational context becomes vendor lock-in ("it looks like convenience until you try to cancel"); Whittemore counters that high switching costs are an unavoidable consequence of AI integration, not something Anthropic-specific, citing Ethan Mollick: AI decisions are now organizational design and strategy decisions, not IT choices.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Anthropic told the Senate Banking Committee that Alibaba accessed its models ~29 million times via 25,000 fraudulent accounts from mid-April to early June — "the largest distillation attack ever detected"; Whittemore notes the "attack" framing is a deliberate rhetorical choice (distillation breaches terms of service, not necessarily law), while Senators Hagerty and King propose blacklisting distilling Chinese labs via the Defense Authorization Act. A Hacker News post described a gray-market economy reselling pooled Claude Max access in China.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Alibaba separately sued the DoD over its Chinese-military-affiliate designation, which analysts see as a possible precursor to a Huawei-style civilian ban.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Google DeepMind departures continue: after Noam Shazeer (to OpenAI) and Nobel laureate John Jumper (to Anthropic), senior Gemini contributors Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel are joining Anthropic; Gemini 3.5 Pro is delayed to July for stress-testing in real-world coding; one Meta researcher noted departures cluster among London DeepMinders as pre-training's center of gravity shifts to Mountain View — and Anthropic opened an 800-person London office in April.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Micron reported 445% year-over-year revenue growth, guided another 22% quarterly jump, disclosed four long-term contracts locking in historically high memory prices at 56% gross margins (forecast to hit 86% in Q4), and rose 14% overnight — reversing a Nasdaq drawdown week; Goldman Sachs warned consensus underestimates the AI build-out by as much as 50%.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- KPMG survey details: "driving adoption" stage jumped from 13% to 22% of organizations; concerns rising include pressure to demonstrate value (19→24%), hiring/upskilling limits (18→22%), and access to lower-cost LLMs (15→22%); 75% say the CEO actively owns AI as a strategic priority.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- Maturity signals: about half of organizations have re-phased AI deployments when costs outweighed value; only ~1/3 have full visibility into AI operating costs; 54% include cost review in AI approvals, 53% have cost dashboards, ~40% have usage or token budgets.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]
- On the human side, 71% of executives report good progress toward an integrated AI-human workforce — but Whittemore notes bosses consistently overestimate employee enthusiasm, and US employee resistance to agents jumped from 5% to 20%, worth watching next quarter.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]

# Takeaways

Whittemore extracts two "quick wins" from the KPMG data: first, make sure everyone knows who is accountable for which AI decisions — clear accountability triples reported ROI; second, "sorry CEOs, it is your job" — CEO ownership dramatically improves established ROI, perceived value, and confidence in future-proofing. Strategically, he urges the two-thirds of organizations lacking cost visibility to build active monitoring before shifting strategy, predicting cost review, dashboards, and token budgets will eventually reach 100% adoption in the token-efficiency era. Overall he reads the survey as evidence organizations are maturing — treating AI as an organizational design challenge rather than a tool-selection problem, and cutting losses on implementations that don't work without abandoning the trajectory.[^ceo-led-ai-gets-3x-the-roi-VICrf_yV0co]

# Overview

OpenAI announced it will begin testing ads in ChatGPT's free and Go tiers, framed by principles of answer independence, clearly labeled ad separation, conversational privacy from advertisers, and no ads on paid tiers — positioning advertising as the way to keep "hyperintelligent AI assistance" accessible to everyone. Nathaniel Whittemore treats the move as always inevitable — only about 5% of users convert to paid, and even at $20B annualized revenue OpenAI burns enormous cash — but sharply criticizes the communication: after Sam Altman once called ads "uniquely unsettling" and a "last resort," and after OpenAI recently dismissed ad sightings as rumors, the company either lied or was in naive denial, "neither case particularly reassuring."[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]

The second half is Whittemore's own strategic pitch (built with Genspark, offered to OpenAI "without attribution") for making ChatGPT ads actually value-additive rather than merely unobtrusive: a user-control foundation plus five categories of innovative ad units, from verified-outcome transactional advertising to founders-grant ads people might root for.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]

# Key points

- OpenAI's stated principles: responses won't be influenced by ads, ads are separate and labeled, conversations stay private from advertisers, Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise stay ad-free, and the company won't optimize for time spent; both Altman and applications CEO Fiji Simo led with "ads will not influence the answers."[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- The bull case (poster "Signal"): Meta made ~$58 per user from ads in 2025; at a billion free users, even 9% of Meta's ARPU is $5B incremental revenue and full parity would be $57B/year — and since AI conversations monetize decisions rather than attention, "social media ARPU may end up looking like a floor, not a ceiling."[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Intent data supports this: one study found ChatGPT traffic converting at 16% versus Google organic's 1.76% — a 9x difference — with other studies consistently showing meaningfully higher intent.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Skeptics invoke the Google playbook (Jason Nume): start with clearly separated ads, wait for habituation, then integrate deeper — "give it 18 months and ChatGPT will be recommending products mid-conversation"; others worry ads raise the manipulation cost of AI memory, and Ben Thompson argues OpenAI should have launched "the world's crappiest ads" back in 2023 so they'd be good by now.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Whittemore's control foundation: let users see and correct what the system believes about their preferences, interact with ads "as partners, not targets" ("I already went"), and control timing ("check back in 3 weeks") — plus a bad-ad policy with flag-and-skip earning an ad-free day, and public advertiser quality ratings where low ratings cost more per impression.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Ad-unit idea 1, transactional: shift from pay-for-attention to pay-for-verified-results ($50 for a completed booking instead of $2 for a click), plus a user-initiated "help me buy" commercial mode with buying agents that negotiate and filter.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Idea 2, offers: an offers exchange with triggered memory ("you said you'd wait for a sale on running shoes — Nike is 30% off today"), negotiated discounts, verified scarcity from real inventory data, and price history context.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Idea 3, brand-funded capabilities: brands sponsor features free users would otherwise hit limits on — e.g., deep research "presented by McKinsey" or a Nike-branded training mode.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Idea 4, branded action agents: the ad is a product, not a placement — opt-in constrained mini-apps inside ChatGPT like an American Express travel concierge or a TurboTax system; Whittemore calls this the most promising area since it proves value in context immediately.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]
- Idea 5, ads people root for: founders grants and small-business ad credits with browsable recipients, creating "Kickstarter type of energy" around the next generation of AI-built businesses.[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes that ads are to some extent a necessary evil — "if you're not paying, you're the product" — but argues OpenAI cannot afford to settle for merely unobtrusive, clearly labeled ads when Google has a 20-year advertising head start. His prescription is an ambitious goal: make ads actually value-additive by exploiting ChatGPT's unique intent signal and conversational context, backed by genuine user control and accountability mechanisms. "Maybe you don't hit the mark, but I think there's value in the attempt."[^how-to-make-chatgpt-ads-not-suck-b7LNP1RZemI]

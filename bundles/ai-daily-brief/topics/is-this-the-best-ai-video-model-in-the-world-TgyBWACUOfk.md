# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore covers the AI world's freakout over ByteDance's Seedance 2.0, a video model released without fanfare that early testers call the most advanced in the world — and possibly the first sign of China innovating ahead of the US rather than just catching up. Its standout feature is native audiovisual generation: unlike competitors that handle audio in post-production, Seedance generates sound alongside video, yielding perfect lip sync and immersive environmental audio, plus 2K resolution, multimodal input, multi-cut 15-second clips, and remarkable character consistency and physics.[^is-this-the-best-ai-video-model-in-the-world-TgyBWACUOfk]

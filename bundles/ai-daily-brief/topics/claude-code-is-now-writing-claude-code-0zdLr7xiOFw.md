# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore says the most interesting story isn't news at all but a milestone in AI coding: Claude Code creator Boris Cherny disclosed that over the previous 30 days, every single line of his contributions to Claude Code — 259 PRs, 497 commits, 40,000 lines added and 38,000 removed — was written by Claude Code running Opus 4.5. Whittemore frames this as confirmation that AI coding's 2025 inflection has carried into a new period in software engineering, citing Ethan Mollick's observation that Dario Amodei's mocked prediction of 90% of code being AI-written was off by only a couple of months, and Andrej Karpathy's viral admission that he has "never felt this much behind as a programmer."[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]

Before that, Whittemore rips through a week of holiday-season news: xAI buying a third building (jokingly named "Macrohard") to push training compute toward almost 2 GW; OpenAI consolidating teams to overhaul its audio models ahead of its Jony Ive-designed consumer device; Nvidia closing its $5 billion Intel investment; and SoftBank and Brookfield making major AI-infrastructure moves, including SoftBank completing its $40 billion OpenAI investment after a scramble for liquidity.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]

# Key points

- xAI purchased a large warehouse in southern Mississippi as a third facility; its Colossus supercluster has around 230,000 GPUs in a single coherent training cluster (the world's largest), with Colossus 2 under construction targeting 550,000 Blackwell GPUs, and 450,000 GPUs operational across facilities.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Musk posted that the third building, called "Macrohard," will take xAI's training compute to almost 2 GW; no hyperscaler has yet completed even a 1-gigawatt data center. xAI is also building its own natural gas power plant, one of the first built specifically for AI infrastructure.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Per The Information, OpenAI consolidated engineering, product, and research teams to overhaul audio models; a new voice-mode model expected in Q1 will sound more natural and emotive, handle interruption better, and can even speak over the user — seen as key to the Jony Ive consumer device expected in about a year, possibly pen-shaped, with manufacturing shifting away from China's Luxshare.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Nvidia closed its $5 billion investment in Intel at $23.28 per share; Intel stock is up 50% since the September announcement, giving Nvidia a roughly 4% stake and a vested interest in reviving Intel's foundry business amid chipmaking capacity constraints.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- SoftBank struck a $4 billion all-cash deal to acquire Digital Bridge, a private equity firm with about $108 billion in digital-infrastructure deals, securing an in-house funding pipeline for its AI projects including Stargate.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- SoftBank confirmed on New Year's Eve it completed its $40 billion OpenAI investment, selling $5.8 billion of Nvidia stock and $4.8 billion of T-Mobile and tapping margin loans against ARM stock to fund the final $22.5 billion payment; it now owns roughly 11% of OpenAI.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Brookfield is spinning off a cloud business tied to its November-launched AI infrastructure fund, capped at $100 billion with $10 billion committed from investors including Nvidia and the Kuwait Investment Authority, developing data centers in France, Qatar, and Sweden.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Boris Cherny: a year ago Claude struggled to generate bash commands without escaping issues; now Claude Code runs for minutes, hours, and days at a time, and in the last 30 days wrote 100% of his Claude Code contributions.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]
- Karpathy described a "new programmable layer of abstraction" — agents, subagents, prompts, context, memory modes, permissions, tools, plugins, skills, hooks, MCP — as a "powerful alien tool" with no manual, advising programmers to "roll up your sleeves to not fall behind."[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]

# Takeaways

Whittemore treats Cherny's disclosure as evidence that software engineering has entered a new period in coding history, less than a year after Karpathy coined "vibe coding" — Claude Code is now good enough to write Claude Code. He notes that xAI's compute buildout matches his 2026 prediction that Grok would break out from the back of the pack via Musk's fundraising and operational prowess, and says Karpathy's advice to roll up your sleeves and claim the productivity boost will be one of the key themes of the AI Daily Brief this year.[^claude-code-is-now-writing-claude-code-0zdLr7xiOFw]

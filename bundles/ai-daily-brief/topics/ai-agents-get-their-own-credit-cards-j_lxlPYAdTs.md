# Overview

In this Headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore leads with Ramp and Stripe both introducing virtual payment cards designed specifically for AI agents — an "obvious in retrospect" development given people were already handing their personal credit cards to agents like Open Claw. He notes the significant detail that both companies chose normal card rails over agent-specific payment protocols like X402 or AP2, because agents have become capable enough to use human-designed payment infrastructure, which also plugs directly into existing merchant systems.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]

The episode rounds up a dense news day: Ramp's AI index shows Anthropic now winning 70% of first-time business AI spending head-to-head against OpenAI; OpenAI plans to fold Sora back into ChatGPT; Musk announces a Tesla–xAI "Macrohard" / Digital Optimus computer-use project; Netflix moves to acquire Ben Affleck's AI startup Interpositive for up to $600 million; and Lovable adds $100 million in annualized revenue in a single month.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]

# Key points

- Ramp's agent cards work via API, MCP, and CLI; card numbers are never exposed, and users get spend limits and real-time transaction visibility. Stripe's version is in limited beta with programmable spending limits, merchant-category controls, and real-time risk scores.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Stripe PM Jeff Weinstein: "we're about to see a lot more agents doing a lot more for us, and that's often going to involve spending money."[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Both firms opted for normal cards rather than agent-focused protocols (X402, AP2) — agent capability advances mean they can use human payment rails, with the structural advantage of existing merchant infrastructure.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Ramp's March AI index: 47.6% of Ramp businesses now have at least one AI subscription; Anthropic is the only company with accelerating adoption at 24.4% of customers versus 34.4% and falling for OpenAI, and Anthropic now wins 70% of first-time AI business spend — a complete reversal of the 2025 trend, per economist Eric Kerzian ("Prophetic is the new default for businesses").[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- OpenAI plans to integrate Sora into ChatGPT; despite Sensor Tower data showing Sora recently passing 3 million DAUs and growing, The Information reports the move refocuses on ChatGPT as the core experience in pursuit of 1 billion weekly active users, partly due to Gemini competition.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Musk announced "Macrohard" / Digital Optimus, a Tesla–xAI joint project: Grok as "system two" conductor directing a Digital Optimus "system one" that processes real-time screen video and keyboard/mouse actions, running on Tesla's low-cost AI4 silicon; Business Insider reports two project leads quit and a data-annotation effort was paused.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Netflix could pay up to $600 million for Ben Affleck's Interpositive — the largest AI media acquisition to date; the tech does custom per-production models for editing shots (lighting, reframing, background replacement), not generating new scenes, and David Fincher is reportedly already using it. Affleck joins Netflix as an advisor.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]
- Lovable's ARR jumped by a third in February from $300 million to $400 million; Cursor doubled ARR to $2 billion in three months. Lovable's debut brand ad never mentions AI or vibe coding.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]

# Takeaways

Whittemore reads the agent-card launches as evidence that agents matured faster than the specialized payment protocols built for them just six months earlier. On the Ramp data, he cautions it is one provider's dataset that may miss purchase-order enterprise deals, but says it fits a broader trend line seen elsewhere. On the Netflix–Interpositive deal, he argues entertainment has a unique opportunity to show AI need not be a humans-versus-robots choice, choosing optimism that such deals enable "more amazing entertainment content enabled by AI that isn't just cost-cutting slop." And with Lovable and Cursor both surging, he concludes "we are very much in a rising tide lifts all boats kind of moment" for vibe coding.[^ai-agents-get-their-own-credit-cards-j_lxlPYAdTs]

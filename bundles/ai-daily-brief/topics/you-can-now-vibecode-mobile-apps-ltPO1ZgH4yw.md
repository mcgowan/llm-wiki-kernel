# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore leads with Replit's new feature that lets users vibe code mobile apps natively and publish them to the App Store "with just a few clicks" — removing the barriers (payments configuration, security auditing, App Store review navigation) that had kept novice builders defaulting to the web. Early beta reports are enthusiastic, with one tester calling a published Replit mobile app "10 out of 10 quality," and Bloomberg reports Replit is closing a roughly $400M round at a $9 billion valuation.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]

The rest of the episode covers video-generation startup Higgsfield's funding at a $1.3B valuation amid what it claims is the fastest-ever run to $200M ARR, an escalating talent exodus at Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab as co-founders return to OpenAI, and Demis Hassabis warning that Chinese AI models may be "only a matter of months behind" US counterparts — while questioning whether Chinese labs can innovate beyond the frontier rather than catch up to it.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]

# Key points

- Replit's mobile feature targets end-to-end publishing: "From idea to app store in minutes, all on Replit," with novice developers completing the whole process without leaving the platform; beta testing had been running since December.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- Bloomberg reports Replit is closing a ~$400M fundraise valuing it at $9 billion.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- Higgsfield, a 9-month-old frontend for open-source video models, extended its Series A by $80M at a $1.3B valuation, claims 15 million users and $200M ARR (doubling from $100M in two months) — which it says makes it the fastest startup to $200M, outpacing Lovable, Cursor, OpenAI, Slack, and Zoom, a claim confirmed as "basically unprecedented" by Anthropic's Henry Shi of the Lean AI leaderboard.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- 85% of Higgsfield usage now comes from social media managers, with fastest adoption among marketers treating generative video as production infrastructure — ideate, storyboard, animate, edit, and publish in one system.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- Thinking Machines Lab has lost three of six co-founders in months: Barret Zoph, Luke Metz, and Sam Schoenholz returning to OpenAI, following Andrew Tulloch's October return to Meta; sources report further resignations after a tense all-hands, with a company source citing "misalignment on what the company wanted to build."[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- Demis Hassabis told CNBC Chinese models are "maybe only a matter of months behind," but argues Chinese labs haven't yet shown they can "innovate something new, like a new transformer that gets beyond the frontier" — inventing being "about 100 times harder" than copying.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]
- Jensen Huang's scorecard: China is "well ahead of us on energy," the US is "way ahead on chips," and China is "right there" on infrastructure and models; Z.ai unveiled its first model trained entirely on Huawei chips and software — a small image model, but proof Huawei now has a fully capable AI development stack.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]

# Takeaways

Whittemore frames the Replit release as a meaningful expansion of vibe coding's reach — the audience is already on mobile, and the App Store barrier has been the main reason builders stayed on the web — and predicts a surge of quality apps in the App Store. On the geopolitical thread, he concludes that Chinese models "are going to do nothing but grow in importance this year," with the impact on the AI race still to be seen.[^you-can-now-vibecode-mobile-apps-ltPO1ZgH4yw]

# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore reviews Claude Design, Anthropic's new design tool released on a Friday midday just after Claude Opus 4.7 — a sign, he notes, of how relentless the release pace has become given the feature moved Figma's stock 7 points. Claude Design wraps design work in a richer UI: Anthropic pitches "rationing exploration" (agentic design removes the need to commit to one direction before prototyping), and refinement happens via natural language, inline comments, direct canvas editing, and per-design custom tweak sliders for elements like spacing, color warmth, and layout tightness — which many, including The Smart Ape, call the killer feature. Notably, Anthropic pitches prototypes, wireframes, mockups, decks, and marketing collateral rather than final production output — unlike Claude Code's full-experience pitch — and images are generated as code and SVGs rather than by an image model.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]

Whittemore's positioning analysis: this is "vibe design," closer to "Figma for non-Figma users" than a Figma replacement, aimed at two audiences — Claude Code power users who aren't designers (design-to-implementation in one conversation) and non-designer knowledge workers, especially marketers. He distinguishes Canva's asset design from Claude Design's systems design (websites, apps, front ends), notes Anthropic CPO Mike Krieger resigned from Figma's board days before launch, and sees the most direct overlap with general agents GenSpark and Manus for code-powered slide and visual design.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]

# Key points

- Claude Design launched the same week as Claude Opus 4.7; Figma shares dropped 7 points on the news, and Mike Krieger had resigned from Figma's board earlier that week.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- The "Socratic design process": after an initial prompt, Claude Design asks refining questions with suggested answers (main role, key flow, number of variations), doubling as product thinking, and lets users delegate any decision back to it.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Per-design generated tweak sliders (spacing, density, color warmth, fonts, interactivity) are what make it "feel like a design tool and not a prompt box with a preview pane" (The Smart Ape).[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Nufar Gaspar praised design-system extraction from an uploaded branded deck (no brand guide needed) and unprompted self-polishing passes, but found exports badly broken — degraded PowerPoints, Canva errors, with HTML the only reliable output.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Early builds: a one-shot Artemis 2 moon-launch site (Mark Dalla Maria), a design agency homepage, Justine Moore's swipe-based dating app front end for X posters, animated social posts, Shopify page variations, and launch/promo videos — all without a separate image model.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Biggest complaints are rate limits — Justine Moore hit Max-plan limits in under 30 minutes; Claude Design has its own usage limit and one user lost a project after burning 10% of usage — plus the SVG-only image limitation (Simon Smith).[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Greg Eisenberg's ratings: wireframing 9/10, mobile app design 8.5/10, deck research and design 8.7/10, video creation 4.5/10.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Whittemore's competitive read: different from asset-first Canva (whose CEO Melanie Perkins is quoted in the launch); Google Stitch is closer to an AI-native Figma; the clearest overlap is with GenSpark and Manus for code-powered slides and visuals.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- His seven things to try: slides (letting Claude reason over raw material), a web project complementing Claude Code, a simple SVG-friendly launch video, brand assets (where it may struggle), a transportable design system built from your best existing work, mobile-app wireframes to see its product thinking, and a Shopify product site.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]
- Community tips: Ryan Mather — "know when to slow down and do things by hand" for high-impact details; The Smart Ape — explicitly ban the default "YC batch aesthetic" (Inter, Roboto, stock blue-purple gradients) to get anything distinctive.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes Claude Design shines most for non-standard, creative projects where rapid iteration across genuinely different directions matters, and that being less prescriptive in prompts (rate limits permitting) often yields better results. He frames it within two bigger questions: which companies end up in Claude's disruption path (less an intentional kill strategy than "they're going to build what they're going to build"), and whether coding's rapid vibe-to-agentic transformation is the template for all knowledge work — noting coding's clear rules and verifiability may make it a special case. For now he sees a vibe-design tool for Claude Coders and marketers rather than a designer's Figma replacement.[^the-best-claude-design-use-cases-bJg5T2qq5dQ]

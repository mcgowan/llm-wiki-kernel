# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore delivers a wide-ranging early-2026 update on the global AI race, opening with his pitch for why it matters even to job-focused listeners: "whether you are interested in the global AI race or not, the global AI race is interested in you" — geopolitics shapes regulation, model competition, and the products everyone gets. A year after the DeepSeek R1 shock wiped nearly $600 billion off Nvidia's market cap in a single day, Chinese labs have moved from cheap fast-followers toward genuine innovation: Moonshot's Kimi K 2.5 is "nipping on the heels" of top Western models, and ByteDance's SeaDance 2.0 video model generates naturalistic sound simultaneously with images — a technical breakthrough Western models haven't matched.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]

Beyond models, Whittemore covers the chips battleground (Huawei's full-stack training proof of concept, H200 exports to China, and bipartisan legislation to ban Blackwell sales), the UAE's bid to be a neutral third AI power serving compute to 4 billion people, China's answer to Musk's orbital data-center ambitions, and domestic US data-center politics including New York's proposed three-year moratorium — which he calls perhaps "the single least effective AI policy for accomplishing its stated goals."[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]

# Key points

- DeepSeek's R1 launch a year prior caused the largest single-day dollar loss in market history (~$600B off Nvidia) and gave most users their first reasoning-model experience, since OpenAI's o1 was paywalled — directly shaping what OpenAI had to do next.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- Demis Hassabis had said Chinese labs were months behind but hadn't shown they could "innovate something new beyond the frontier" — a claim Whittemore says SeaDance 2.0 and Kimi K 2.5 now call into question.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- A wave of Lunar New Year releases is expected from Alibaba, Baidu, and ByteDance; Zhipu (rebranded Z.AI) is rumored to be behind the "Pony Alpha" stealth model on OpenRouter; DeepSeek V4 is widely rumored for the New Year after a year of V3 iterations.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- Alibaba's Qwen technical lead Justin Lin told CNN the odds of China overtaking the US are "below 20%, and I think 20% is already very optimistic."[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- JP Morgan issued buy signals for Zhipu and MiniMax; in China new model launches lift the broader software, filmmaking, and online-literature sectors — a stark contrast with the US, where Anthropic and Gemini releases wiped hundreds of billions off software stocks.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- Zhipu trained its first model exclusively on Huawei chips — unimpressive as a model but a zero-to-one proof that Huawei has a complete hardware/software training stack; meanwhile Nvidia H200s are fully approved for China export, with labs reportedly ordering hundreds of thousands.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- Senators Elizabeth Warren and Jim Banks plan bipartisan legislation including a two-year outright ban on Blackwell exports and a congressional veto over export approvals; Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei was reportedly crucial to getting the bill moving and met with Warren.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- G42 CEO Peng Zhao unveiled "the world's largest AI chip" (using Cerebras wafer-scale manufacturing) at the World Government Summit in Dubai, framing the UAE as a neutral third party aiming to serve compute to the 4 billion people between Milan and Singapore; G42 also plans a billion-dollar Vietnam data center.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- Semafor reports OpenAI is in talks with G42 on a UAE-specific ChatGPT tuned for local language, political views, and speech restrictions — possibly as a compromise to block G42 from proliferating Chinese models.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- After Musk's space data-center ambitions were met with Western derision, China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation announced plans for "gigawatt class space digital intelligence infrastructure" within 5 years and vowed to make China the dominant space power by 2045; Musk is reportedly sounding out Chinese solar suppliers.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]
- New York lawmakers proposed a three-year data-center moratorium (echoing Bernie Sanders' federal call), but Politico polling shows most voters are blasé or mildly positive about local data centers — only 32% of Democrats oppose them, versus 25% of Republicans.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes the AI race is no longer a simple two-superpower contest: neutral actors like the UAE are staking claims, the competition now spans models, chips, data centers, and even orbit, and every move in DC or Beijing shows up in the models and policies users experience in San Francisco. On domestic policy he argues moratoria are self-defeating — restricting compute supply is "the single fastest way to ensure that access is limited to only the people who can pay" — while still holding that governments should shape the build-out, particularly because making local consumers foot the bill for grid expansion is "completely inappropriate" in this context.[^how-the-global-ai-race-has-changed-ozxH7SS0Svw]

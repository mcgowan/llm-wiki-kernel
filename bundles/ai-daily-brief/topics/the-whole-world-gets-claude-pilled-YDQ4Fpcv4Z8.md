# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore reports that the capability shift centered on Opus 5, Claude Code, and Claude Co-work has broken out of the enfranchised AI crowd on X and into mainstream press, with both the Wall Street Journal and The Atlantic running enthusiastic features on non-technical people building software and automating personal tasks with Claude Code. He contrasts that enthusiasm with a Google-commissioned Ipsos survey showing the United States ranking last among 21 countries in both AI usage and optimism.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]

He also covers Anthropic's supersized funding round, xAI's Colossus 2 crossing a gigawatt of capacity, and the damages figure Elon Musk's lawyers put on his suit against OpenAI and Microsoft ahead of an April trial.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]

# Key points

- The Wall Street Journal described "getting Claude-pilled" as the moment engineers, executives, and investors hand work to Claude and see a thinking machine of surprising capability, noting non-technical users writing their first software and applying Claude Code to health data analysis and expense reports.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- The Atlantic's writer used vibe coding tools for the first time for the piece, built a personal website in minutes, and spun up a dozen more projects within days; a profiled user, Alex Lieberman, argued the implications exceed the ChatGPT moment while noting Pandora's box hasn't opened for the rest of the world yet.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- Claude Code creator Boris Cherney called it an overnight success years in the making; Anjney Midha noted the Journal's front page was about everyday people using a command line interface and urged business leaders to revisit their operating assumptions.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- Anthropic's reported round at a $350 billion valuation is being supersized to potentially $25 billion — roughly $15 billion from Microsoft and Nvidia plus $10 billion from VCs including Sequoia.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- The Ipsos survey of about 1,000 adults in each of 21 countries found 66% had used AI in the past 12 months, up from 48% in 2024 and 28% in 2023; respondents split 50-50 on whether AI creates or eliminates jobs, but 58% favored fostering AI advancement over protecting disrupted industries.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- Optimism tracked usage: 70% of AI users were optimistic and 86% of heavy users excited. The US was the only country without a majority of AI users at 40%, versus 56% in the UK, 66% in Mexico, and north of 80% in the UAE, Nigeria, and India; only 33% of US respondents were mostly excited, the worst result against a 57% overall average.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- xAI's Colossus 2 reached 1 gigawatt, the first training cluster to cross that threshold, drawing more power than the city of San Francisco and reportedly running 550,000 Blackwell GPUs after nine months of construction. Colossus 1 totals 300MW; OpenAI disclosed 1.9GW across its entire fleet; Anthropic and Amazon's Carlyle data center is expected to reach 1GW in Q1, and OpenAI's Stargate Abilene over the summer.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- Musk's lawyers quantified damages at up to $134 billion, arguing his $38 million in 2015 seed funding entitles him to a share of OpenAI's $500 billion valuation, plus punitive damages and an injunction; OpenAI called the methodology "made up" and the suit baseless harassment. Trial is set for late April.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]
- The filing surfaced private notes and Altman's account that Musk sought majority equity and full control, discussed accumulating $80 billion for a self-sustaining Mars city, and raised his children controlling AGI in a succession conversation.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]

# Takeaways

Whittemore treats America's low AI usage and optimism as a major national issue, arguing the stakes are not the user counts of OpenAI or Anthropic but whether people take the coming disruption seriously enough to prepare — and he says a strand of people hoping to wait it out and return to the prior world will be disappointed. On compute he notes xAI is alone at this scale for now, which he has previously flagged as the differentiation opportunity for Grok in the year ahead. On the Musk litigation he expects it to overhang OpenAI through a pivotal year and to make many people look greedy, and states he will cover only the major inescapable developments rather than turn the show into court drama.[^the-whole-world-gets-claude-pilled-YDQ4Fpcv4Z8]

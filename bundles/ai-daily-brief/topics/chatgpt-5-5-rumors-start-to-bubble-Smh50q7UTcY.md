# Overview

Two weeks into 2026, Nathaniel Whittemore surveys a heating-up model rumor mill, starting with context: OpenAI's rocky five months from the inauspicious GPT-5 launch (soured by the 4o deprecation and naming choices) through Google's triumphant Gemini 3 / Nano Banana Pro moment, Sam Altman's internal "code red" memo, and the December releases of GPT-5.2 and a new ChatGPT Images model that only partially stemmed the bleeding. Meanwhile Claude Opus 4.5's reputation "has done nothing but go up" — Whittemore repeats his call that it may be the most important model release of 2025 — and Anthropic's Cowork launch plus the Google-Apple deal (Apple Intelligence powered by Gemini) have left momentum with Anthropic and Google while OpenAI seems "conspicuously quiet."[^chatgpt-5-5-rumors-start-to-bubble-Smh50q7UTcY]

Against that backdrop, leakers report a new ChatGPT model code-named "garlic" — possibly GPT-5.3 or GPT-5.5 — with stronger pre-training and IMO-gold reasoning techniques, with claims that GPT-5.2 was a "rushed early checkpoint." The episode also covers a supply-chain leak on OpenAI's AirPods-replacing audio device "Sweet Pea," reports that DeepSeek V4 arrives mid-February with frontier coding claims, Google's Veo 3.1 ingredients-to-video feature, and Anthropic Labs expanding into a full internal incubator.[^chatgpt-5-5-rumors-start-to-bubble-Smh50q7UTcY]

# Takeaways

Whittemore frames the moment as one where the narrative momentum sits with Anthropic and Google while OpenAI regroups post-code-red — though he allows that OpenAI may simply be vague-posting less in 2026, and notes the ChatGPT Health launch as a counterpoint to the "quiet" framing. He'd welcome "a big powerful effort" that shakes the race up again, observes that the holiday season left model users spoiled for choice regardless of the horse race, and reiterates his prior thesis that ear-worn devices could be the most obvious under-appreciated AI form factor — making OpenAI's Sweet Pea exploration notable.

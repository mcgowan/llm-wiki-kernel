# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore covers what he calls a week that reset many people's sense of where AI stands, driven by a cascade of Anthropic news. Andrej Karpathy — OpenAI co-founding team member, former Tesla Vision director, and coiner of "vibe coding" — announced he was joining Anthropic's pre-training team to build a group using Claude to accelerate pre-training research itself, a move many observers read as a signal that recursive self-improvement (RSI) is now in sight for the frontier labs. The same week, financials shared with investors showed Anthropic forecasting a $559M operating profit on $10.9B Q2 revenue (a $44B annualized rate) — the first profitable quarter for any foundation lab — and Anthropic's SpaceX partnership deepened, with a $45B three-year Colossus contract making Anthropic SpaceX's largest revenue source.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]

In the headlines, the Wall Street Journal reported OpenAI has engaged bankers and could file confidential IPO paperwork within days, targeting September readiness — likely leapfrogging Anthropic's reported October target; the White House briefed lab CEOs on a coming AI executive order establishing voluntary pre-release model disclosure to government; OpenAI launched a Guaranteed Capacity program letting enterprises lock in one-to-three-year compute commitments; and Nvidia delivered record earnings of $81.6B revenue with data center growth of 92%.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]

# Key points

- The Wall Street Journal reported OpenAI expects to file confidential IPO paperwork as soon as Friday, aiming to be IPO-ready by September, with the resolution of the Elon Musk lawsuit clearing the way; Anthropic, assembling one last private round, is unlikely to move up its October target.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- A reported White House executive order would create a voluntary framework for pre-release model disclosure — the White House wanting models shared 90 days ahead of release, labs pushing for 14 days — plus a Pentagon directive to harden critical systems in 30 days, a Treasury-run AI clearing house, and NSA-run classified model testing on a 60-day preparation timeline.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- OpenAI's new Guaranteed Capacity program offers enterprises one-to-three-year compute commitments with discounts and priority for critical workflows, making AI billing look more like cloud than SaaS — and, Whittemore notes, providing rock-solid ARR numbers heading into an IPO; OpenAI is also giving every current Y Combinator startup $2M in tokens for equity.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Cursor's Composer 2.5 ranked third on Artificial Analysis's coding agent index — behind Opus 47 Max and GPT 5.5 extra-high but ahead of both at medium settings — at 10-60x lower cost, relevant as token efficiency becomes an enterprise constraint.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Andrej Karpathy announced he joined Anthropic; Nicholas Joseph of Anthropic said Karpathy will build a pre-training team "focused on using Claude to accelerate pre-training research itself." Observers noted the pattern: Jan Leike, John Schulman, and now Karpathy have all moved from OpenAI to Anthropic.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Commentators like TMT Long Shot and Shanu Matthew read the hire as evidence the labs believe recursive self-improvement is close, implying compute value "explodes" as demand creation turns non-linear; Swyx declared "RSI is here." Dario Amodei had told Dwarkesh in February that AI-assisted AI research compounding was previously too small to matter — but that is changing.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Anthropic forecasts $10.9B in Q2 revenue (up from $4.8B last quarter), a $44B annualized rate, and a $559M operating profit — the first profitable quarter of any foundation lab, years ahead of its own 2029 forecast; caveats include revenue accounting before partner shares and the fact that sold-out compute prevents Anthropic from spending more even if it wanted to.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Derek Thompson argued Anthropic's revenue could be $100B+ with sufficient inference compute; Gavin Baker contrasted today's structurally constrained wafer and power supply with the late-90s telco bubble's quick supply response, arguing there is no comparable slack now.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Nvidia posted record earnings: $81.6B revenue vs $78.9B expected, EPS of $1.87 vs $1.67, data center revenue up 92% year-over-year, first full Blackwell quarter, and — newly broken out — 46% of data center revenue from hyperscalers with Nvidia gaining share against Google TPUs; Jensen Huang confirmed zero chip sales in China, saying "we've evacuated that market" to Huawei. The stock still fell 3% after hours, which Patrick Moorhead attributed to investors not knowing how to price a $5T+ company.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Anthropic Chief Compute Officer Tom Brown announced GB200 scale-up in SpaceX's Colossus 2 through June; the SpaceX IPO filing revealed Anthropic agreed to pay $45B over three years (~$15B/year) for Colossus — instantly SpaceX's biggest revenue line, exceeding Starlink's $11B and adding 80% to SpaceX revenue on last year's figures.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]
- Musk tweeted that SpaceX is now "offering AI compute as a service at significant scale," with orbital data centers ahead; Signal called the pairing "really, really bad news" for OpenAI since its most profitable competitor now has access to potentially unlimited compute it can pay for in cash.[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes the week amounts to an expectations reset: Anthropic's profitability at a $44B run rate "pretty dramatically challenges the math once again for AI skeptics," whose latest goalpost had been that profitability at scale was impossible. He admits his own 2026 prediction that neither Anthropic nor OpenAI would go public this year is clearly wrong, with compute constraints making public market access more important than expected. The deeper signal, in his telling, is the RSI thesis — Karpathy's recursive-research mandate, labs reorganizing around coding, and billions spent "token maxing" all suggest the build-better-AI-to-build-better-AI loop is beginning to compound, which could break the familiar pattern of state-of-the-art rotating between labs. Connor Sen's capstone: "The Anthropic IPO won't be for less than $2 trillion."[^anthropic-just-reset-ai-expectations-9N3jEavj5Ps]

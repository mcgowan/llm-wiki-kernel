# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore leads this headlines edition with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's remarks at Wednesday's Morgan Stanley TMT conference: "OpenClaw is probably the single most important release of software probably ever. Linux took some 30 years to reach this level. OpenClaw in... 3 weeks has now surpassed Linux" — a reference to the viral GitHub-star-history chart showing OpenClaw overtaking Linux and React faster than any project on record. Whittemore stresses the context: OpenClaw has been "ground zero in ushering in the true agent era," and Huang making the case at a Wall Street conference signals to investors that personal agents are a big deal — an update to his long-running "token economy" thesis that AI tokens are the new fundamental unit of work and GDP.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]

The episode also covers China's explosive OpenClaw adoption, the tit-for-tat ARR leaks between Anthropic and OpenAI, and Google's new cinematic video overviews in NotebookLM, which Whittemore reads as one of Google's biggest multimodal flexes yet.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]

# Key points

- Huang said the rumored $100 billion OpenAI investment is "probably not in the cards" — not from reduced bullishness but because his base case is an OpenAI IPO by year-end, making Nvidia's recent $30 billion investment possibly "the last time we'll have the chance to invest in a consequential company like this"; Nvidia's $10 billion Anthropic investment late last year was also probably its last, while Amazon's compute partnership with OpenAI has Nvidia "ramping AWS like mad."[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- The Information reports OpenClaw is reshaping Chinese startup-building: a hackathon produced "Tinder for AI agents," an automated recruiting site where job seekers' and companies' OpenClaws interview each other, and a gamified social/travel platform hosting OpenClaw-created content.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- Unlike Western cloud giants, ByteDance, Alibaba, and Tencent all offer hosted OpenClaw instances, and Moonshot and MiniMax bundle cloud OpenClaw into their apps; competition is so fierce that Chinese OpenClaw installers throw in two hours of house cleaning for $57.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- Lenny Rachitsky: over 80 OpenClaw meetups scheduled worldwide for a product a few months old — "I've never seen anything like this. Something very special is happening."[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- The ARR leak war: Bloomberg reported Anthropic surpassed $19 billion ARR (more than doubling since year-end, including a 36% gain in a couple of weeks); The Information countered that OpenAI now exceeds $25 billion ARR after ending 2025 at $21.4 billion — a 17% jump in two months — and extrapolating just the past week would put it at $30 billion.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- Derek Thompson's framing: AI might still be an industrial bubble with revenue far behind CapEx, "but the idea that this industry has no business model is a take aging like a rotted banana."[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]
- NotebookLM's cinematic video overviews orchestrate the Gemini 3 family (Nano Banana Pro and Veo) with Gemini acting as "creative director" making hundreds of structural and stylistic decisions; Robert Scoble's example — a nearly 5-minute video summarizing AI chatter on X — was generated almost entirely inside NotebookLM, though the feature is exclusive to the top-tier Ultra subscription.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's throughlines: Huang's OpenClaw superlative matters less for its literal accuracy than for where it was delivered — Wall Street being told to get up to speed on personal agents as the token economy "comes into focus." The Chinese ecosystem's instant, whole-of-industry response to OpenClaw ("nobody wants to be left behind") shows the agent era going global with big platforms moving faster than their Western counterparts. And the dueling revenue leaks confirm the business-model skeptics are losing the argument, even if bubble questions linger. He flags NotebookLM's cinematic overviews as something he'll return to with a dedicated operator-focused episode.[^jensen-huang-calls-openclaw-most-important-softw-lquuveY5i-g]

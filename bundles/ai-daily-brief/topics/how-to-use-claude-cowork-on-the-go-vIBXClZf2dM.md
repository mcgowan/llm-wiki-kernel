# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore leads with Anthropic's new Dispatch feature for Claude Co-work — a seemingly small release that drew an outsized reaction (3 million views, 9,000 bookmarks on the announcement tweet), which he reads as a signal of what actually matters to AI builders. Dispatch lets users kick off Co-work sessions hosted in a sandbox on their computer, then monitor progress and provide approvals from their phone — Anthropic's "walkie-talkie for communicating with Claude" — continuing the pattern of Anthropic folding Open Claude's most important features into its product suite rather than cloning it.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]

The rest of the episode tracks a China-heavy news cycle: government warnings against Open Claude at state agencies, an AI-anxiety spike on WeChat, Beijing's second look at Meta's Manus acquisition, Nvidia restarting H200 production for China, and Amazon CEO Andy Jassy's projection that AI could double AWS to $600 billion in annual revenue.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]

# Key points

- Dispatch extends the earlier "remote control for Claude Code" pattern ("coding from the gym") to Co-work: sessions stay sandboxed on your computer with the same access and protections, controllable from anywhere; Co-work developer Philipp Reisberg described getting updates on missions like "finding me a better seat on my next flight."[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Ethan Mollick: "Dispatch covers 90% of what I was trying to use Open Claude for, but feels far less likely to upload my entire drive to a malware site" — praising stability, safety, connectors, and tool use, while missing channel invites, heartbeat/proactivity, and multiple sessions (Dispatch is one chat).[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Whittemore endorses Paul Hearn's framing: Code, Co-work, Web, and Dispatch "are all converging towards the same thing, a persistent AI layer that follows you across devices and contexts" — the "Claude-ification" trend as form-factor experimentation.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Chinese regulators warned government agencies and state-owned enterprises against installing Open Claude, issued six dos and don'ts for organizational deployment, and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority formally stated it has no deployment plans; Chinese media runs horror stories including a credit card run up to its limit.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Stanford's Grant Webster suggests Open Claude could be a flashpoint prompting the Chinese government to rethink its permissive "AI Plus" stance toward open models; analyst Wendy Cheng attributes its popularity to Chinese consumers' willingness to try new technology plus weak software-subscription culture making free open source attractive.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- "AI anxiety" spiked on WeChat from February through mid-March as Open Claude mania crested; Tony Peng of Abacus China notes the mood shifted from excitement to fear, driven by job insecurity and the gap between chatbot-level familiarity and reports of autonomous agents.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- China's National Development and Reform Commission summoned Meta and Manus executives over the acquisition — Manus having moved its HQ from Beijing to Singapore — with options reportedly including barring executive departures, clawing back data exports, or declaring the relocation unlawful; Meta insists the deal "complied fully with applicable law."[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Jensen Huang announced Nvidia is restarting manufacturing for licensed Chinese customers after on-again/off-again H200 export approvals; Reuters sources confirm Chinese approval for multiple purchasers, with firms ordering hundreds of thousands of chips — supporting Huang's forecast of a trillion dollars in sales by 2027.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]
- Andy Jassy told an AWS all-hands that AI could lift AWS to $600B annual revenue long-term — double his prior $300B estimate — versus $128B booked in 2025; analyst Patrick Moorhead calls it "the clearest signal yet that hyperscale cloud is entering a second growth phase."[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's read on Dispatch is that its missing features would be dealbreakers for hardcore Open Claude users, but that's not the point — the point is bringing agent feature sets to the full spectrum of users, with all the major products converging on a persistent cross-device AI layer. On China, he senses "an interesting story brewing" in the tension between mass Open Claude enthusiasm and government alarm, though he admits he's not yet sure what it says about where things stand — flagging it as one to keep watching, alongside evidence that job-displacement fear persists even in the world's most AI-optimistic market.[^how-to-use-claude-cowork-on-the-go-vIBXClZf2dM]

# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore examines Tim Cook stepping down as Apple CEO after 15 years, with hardware chief John Ternus taking over, and what it means for Apple's strange AI trajectory: years of inaction under LLM-skeptic AI lead John Giannandrea, an Apple Intelligence launch in mid-2024 that delivered none of its promised features (including the updated Siri everyone wanted), and then an accidental renaissance in 2026 as the agentic era made the Mac Mini the sold-out device of choice for Open Claw setups and leading AI products became Mac-first. Some commentators argue Apple "nailed AI by doing nothing" — $135B in the bank, Gemini powering Siri for a "measly" $1B, competitors forced to plug into 2.5 billion Apple users — while critics like Polymath argue Apple squandered the most compelling pre-AI position (Siri, user data, its own silicon) and that Cook's 11x market-cap growth trailed every other big-tech peer.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]

Whittemore is skeptical the do-nothing strategy was intentional, but notes Ternus inherits AI as his defining question. The episode rounds out Big Tech's AI reset: Google's DeepMind "strike team" (with Sergey Brin directly involved) to close the agentic coding gap with Anthropic, Amazon's $25B investment in Anthropic paid substantially in Trainium compute, and Meta's reported 10% headcount reduction alongside its Level Up fiber-technician training program.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]

# Key points

- Tim Cook steps down after transforming Apple from $350B to $4T over 15 years, though skeptics note Microsoft did 14x, Google 20x, Amazon 28x, and Facebook 35x over the same period; Cook stays on as executive chairman.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- John Ternus, at Apple since 2001 and head of hardware, was chosen over product lead Craig Federighi — who, per one anonymous former Apple leader, "fumbled the bag on AI and Siri."[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Mark Gurman's sources frame decisiveness as the key Cook/Ternus difference: "If you go to Tim with A or B, he won't pick... With Ternus, it could be right or wrong, but at least it's a decision."[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- The Open Claw agentic wave made the Mac Mini sell out nationwide as dedicated agent hardware; Max Weinbach: "If you don't have a Mac and are trying to keep up with the cutting-edge AI, you literally can't."[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Apple announced this year that Gemini will power Siri, which commentator Ejaz framed as Apple stealing "Google's model for a measly 1 billion" while sitting on $135B in cash; Chicago Booth's Alex E Mac argues waiting out the race was Apple's intentional hardware-first strategy.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- The Information lists Ternus's three big questions: fixing AI strategy, managing the transition with Cook as executive chairman, and decoupling from Chinese suppliers; the FT called it his "defining AI moment."[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Google created a strike team, with Sergey Brin directly involved, to close the agentic coding gap; Brin's memo: "To win the final sprint, we must urgently bridge the gap in agentic execution, and turn our models into primary developers" — emphasizing models trained on Google's own private codebase for internal use.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Contrast: Anthropic's Boris Cherny says essentially 100% of Anthropic's code is now AI-written, versus about half of Google's per CFO Anat Ashkenazi's February earnings call.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Amazon committed $25B to Anthropic ($5B now, $20B tied to milestones) atop $8B over the prior 18 months and its $50B OpenAI commitment; the deal functions like Anthropic paying for 5 GW of Trainium compute with equity, with 1 GW expected online by year-end to ease Anthropic's inference shortage.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]
- Meta reportedly plans a 10% headcount cut (~8,000 workers) starting in May, with more expected later in the year, while launching Level Up — a free 4-week fiber-technician training program with CBRE feeding Meta's data-center construction contractor network.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]

# Takeaways

Whittemore lands between the extremes: he doubts Apple's AI restraint was masterful strategy rather than "the default result of doing nothing, which happened to work out," but acknowledges the position Ternus inherits is stronger than it looked a year ago. The market is cautiously optimistic while the AI industry stays skeptical; with WWDC and a new iPhone cycle ahead, he argues simply shipping "an actually good Siri would go a long way." On Meta's trades program, his verdict is "yes, more of this, please" — the AI industry benefits from showing the creation side of creative destruction.[^how-apple-s-ai-strategy-changes-with-a-new-ceo-XwdLT7qF14E]

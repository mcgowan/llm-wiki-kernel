# Overview

In this bonus "operators" episode tied to his Agent Madness bracket competition (agentmadness.ai), Nathaniel Whittemore stages a 16-entry, March Madness-style tournament among the projects he has built with AI so far in the year — all vibe-coded or AI-assisted, many agentic. He frames the exercise around a broader observation: over the prior three to four months there has been a "massive agentic shift," with tools like Open Claw, Claude Code, Codex, and Perplexity Computer getting "everyone agentified." He seeded the bracket by giving both Claude and ChatGPT the project list (they produced nearly identical seedings) and judged matchups on technical complexity, daily-life usefulness, value beyond himself, and x-factor.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]

The strongest entrants belong to a Sherlock Holmes-themed ecosystem of advisory agents Whittemore describes as a next-generation, agentified version of what his company Superintelligent does: Holmes (individual AI-use recommendations via web and Slack), Mycroft (a company-wide "digital chief AI officer"), and 221B (the agentic knowledge hub powering both). After Mycroft defeats finalist Chucky — an "agent builder's representative" inspired by Good Will Hunting that presents a builder's portfolio interactively — Mycroft is crowned tournament champion.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]

# Key points

- The bracket features 16 projects built during the year, seeded by Claude and ChatGPT (which produced almost identical seedings), and judged on technical complexity, usefulness, value to others, and x-factor.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- Holmes interviews individuals about their work and AI usage via web and Slack, builds per-person "case files" (identity, daily work, AI profile, working style), and refreshes its tool recommendations weekly by pulling from the 221B knowledge hub.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- Mycroft — named for Sherlock's smarter older brother — builds an entire company's AI roadmap covering use cases, data and systems integration, governance, upskilling, and ROI goals; Whittemore calls it "your digital chief AI officer."[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- 221B is the agentic knowledge base behind both agents: it ingests show transcripts, runs automated web searches, interviews Whittemore weekly, and assembles dossiers on what matters in enterprise AI.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- Chucky, inspired by Ben Affleck's character in Good Will Hunting, is an agent that represents an agent builder to prospective clients — interactively presenting projects, screenshots, and a portfolio view — which Whittemore thinks could become a jobs-matching form factor.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- AIDB New Year, a self-directed 10-week AI upskilling program launched as a near-throwaway idea, drew more than 7,000 participants and inspired the follow-up Clawhammer program for learning Open Claw.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- The Witty RADARS researcher — his most useful Open Claw agent — hunts 24/7 for studies and surveys to feed a use-case database that tiers use cases as Primetime, Emerging, or Frontier.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- Other entrants include a Lovable-built AI Daily Brief website, an Open Claw coder ("witty builder") for vibe-coding via Telegram, a Perplexity Computer-built AI research library, Model Mog (model-preference matchups; power users average ~3.5 models), a Mission Control Center for monitoring 10+ agents, an Open Claw chief of staff over six project-manager agents, Maturity Maps (a six-vector quarterly benchmark he positions against the Gartner Magic Quadrant), and the Superintelligent Compass platform.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]
- His Open Claw setup runs off a Mac mini; he notes the chief-of-staff agents underperformed because he never wired them into systems like Slack, leaving them as "externalized to-do lists" competing with Notion.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]

# Takeaways

Whittemore crowns Mycroft champion because it doesn't just collect information — it does something valuable with it in a customized, continuously improving way, and he believes it may be the best mechanism he has found to scale how he helps people figure out AI. He also flags Chucky as a possible form factor for the future of presenting one's work, and predicts persistent, continuously updated agentic advisory systems will replace one-time consulting-style AI assessments. More broadly, he treats the sheer volume of builds as evidence of the agentic shift: even the failed or shelved experiments taught him which tools and workflows actually matter.[^the-16-coolest-agents-i-ve-built-so-far-CnxQpf02_js]

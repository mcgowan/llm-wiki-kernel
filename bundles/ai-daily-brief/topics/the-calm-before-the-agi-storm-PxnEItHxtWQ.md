# Overview

Back from spring break, Nathaniel Whittemore surveys a week without a single blockbuster story and argues the sum of the news adds up to something bigger — "the calm before the AGI storm," an electric-charge feeling of the big labs jostling and positioning for a fast-moving future. OpenAI dominated the week: a record $122 billion round closed at an $852 billion valuation, $2 billion in monthly revenue, executive reshuffles including Mira Murati's medical leave, reported Altman-Friar tension over IPO timing and spending, and the controversial acquisition of tech talk show TBPN.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]

Anthropic meanwhile had a rough week — an accidental leak of 512,000 lines of Claude Code source, a botched wave of GitHub takedowns, a usage-limits backlash, and a decision to cut subscription access for third-party tools like OpenClaw — while Google released the open-source Gemma 4 family, Alibaba's Qwen 3.6 validated its pivot to proprietary models, DeepSeek V4 loomed on Huawei silicon, Microsoft restarted in-house model training, and the Iran war and equipment shortages clouded data-center buildouts. Whittemore closes on rumored next-generation models and OpenAI's new "social contract" document as signs the storm is near.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]

# Key points

- OpenAI closed a $122B round at an $852B valuation — $110B announced in February from Amazon, Nvidia, and SoftBank plus $12B more from mostly financial investors, including ~$3B from individuals via wealth-management channels; its stock will appear in Ark Invest ETFs, and monthly revenue is $2B, up from ~$1.6B at year-end.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Bloomberg reports OpenAI shares are struggling in secondary markets — Next Round Capital's Ken Smythe couldn't place hundreds of millions in shares — while buyers hold ~$2B ready to deploy into Anthropic, whose shares trade at up to a $600B implied valuation versus its last $380B raise.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- OpenAI executive shuffle: CEO of AGI deployment Mira Murati took medical leave for a chronic neuroimmune condition; Greg Brockman takes product, with Jason Kwon, Sarah Friar, and Denise Dresser covering business functions; Brad Lightcap moved to special projects and Katie Rausch stepped back for cancer recovery, with ex-Meta CMO Gary Briggs filling in.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- The Information reports Sam Altman wants a Q4 IPO — possibly ahead of Anthropic's October target — while CFO Sarah Friar doubts readiness and is skeptical of the $600B five-year infrastructure commitment and a forecast $200B burn before profitability late in the decade; Altman has reportedly excluded her from some financial conversations.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- OpenAI acquired daily tech talk show TBPN with strict editorial independence in the terms; Whittemore reads it as OpenAI buying "good media as usual" but doubts it addresses their real problem — the narrative outside tech circles, where TBPN has little reach.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Anthropic accidentally shipped 512,000 lines of Claude Code source, then wrongly DMCA'd over 8,000 GitHub repos (mostly innocent forks) before apologizing; the leak revealed an always-on background agent called Kairos with dream and proactive modes, a Tamagotchi-like "Buddies" duck feature, five compaction strategies, and deeply non-trivial harness engineering.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Claude usage-limit complaints hit a fever pitch; Anthropic's Lidia Hallie attributed it to tighter peak-hour limits and bigger million-token sessions, advising users to switch to Sonnet and start fresh sessions — a "you're holding it wrong" answer critics like Alex Volkov found unsatisfying.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Anthropic ended subscription access for third-party tools like OpenClaw (API-only now); Daniel Jeffries argued the subsidy era is ending and true agent intelligence will cost closer to human salaries — a point Whittemore flags as hugely consequential for the jobs conversation.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Google released Gemma 4 in four sizes (2B, 4B, 26B MoE, 31B dense); the 31B ranks third among open-source models on Arena behind Kimi K2.5 and GLM 5 — the first Western open-source model competing at this level in years, runnable locally with a 256K context window.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Alibaba released three proprietary models in three days culminating in Qwen 3.6 — near-Opus 4.5 performance at ~1/8 the cost, million-token context — which hit number one on OpenRouter and became the first model to serve a trillion tokens on release day, validating its revenue-first pivot.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- DeepSeek V4 is expected within weeks, delayed by last-minute optimization for Huawei chips; Chinese tech giants have ordered hundreds of thousands of the new Huawei units, a potential watershed for China's semiconductor self-sufficiency.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Microsoft released three small models (transcription, voice, image) for internal cost-cutting; Mustafa Suleyman, now focused solely on model training, targets state-of-the-art "certainly by 2027" on a new GB200 Blackwell cluster, and Copilot hit Q1 sales goals.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- The Iranian Revolutionary Guard declared 18 US tech companies "legitimate targets" after drone strikes on three Amazon data centers in Bahrain and the UAE; separately, more than half of US data centers face delays from shortages of transformers, switchgear, and batteries.[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]
- Three mystery models ("masking tape," "gaffer tape," "packing tape") on Arena may preview OpenAI's Spud, its first LLM with native multimodal training; OpenAI also dropped a "social contract" document opening: "As we move towards superintelligence, incremental policy updates won't be enough."[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's conclusion is that the quiet week is deceptive: fundraising records, IPO maneuvering, pricing resets, open-versus-closed strategy shifts, and geopolitical shocks all read as labs bracing for a major capability jump. He gives weight to Jeffries' warning that agent economics may resemble salaries rather than pennies, treats the OpenAI-Anthropic IPO race as the axis of the year, and sees the rumored next model generation — plus OpenAI's superintelligence social-contract framing — as evidence that "we are on the verge of something much bigger."[^the-calm-before-the-agi-storm-PxnEItHxtWQ]

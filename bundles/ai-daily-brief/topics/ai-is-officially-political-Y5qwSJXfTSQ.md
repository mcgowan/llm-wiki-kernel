# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore argues that AI has crossed from flirting with politics into being a distinctly political issue — The Verge goes further, calling it part of the culture wars. The catalyst is the Anthropic–Pentagon fight: Anthropic refused to drop contract red lines around domestic surveillance and autonomous weapons, enraging Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, prompting Trump to declare Anthropic persona non grata on Truth Social and the Pentagon to pursue a "supply chain risk" designation while pushing other defense contractors to drop the company — on the same day OpenAI announced its own Department of War deal.[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]

The episode centers on Dario Amodei's leaked 1,600-word Friday-night memo, which accused OpenAI of "mendacious" messaging, called its military safeguards "maybe 20% real and 80% safety theater," and charged that the administration's real grievance is that Anthropic hasn't donated to Trump, hasn't offered "dictator style praise," has supported AI regulation, and has held its red lines. Whittemore weighs whether the memo was strategic — recruitment or App Store politics — or simply a CEO "on mega tilt."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]

# Key points

- Anthropic's negotiations reportedly collapsed over a single clause: the Pentagon agreed to everything but required deleting the phrase about "analysis of bulk acquired data" — which Amodei called "exactly the scenario we were most worried about."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- On autonomous weapons, Amodei noted human-in-the-loop is only Pentagon policy from the Biden era, changeable at will by Hegseth — "not, for all intents and purposes, a real constraint."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Amodei accused Sam Altman of bad faith — publicly supporting Anthropic while undercutting it ("I want people to recognize this as the gaslighting it is") — and worried the narrative would work on OpenAI employees, whom he called "sort of a gullible bunch."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Anthropic's app hit number two on the App Store amid anti-administration sentiment; interpretations of the memo split between strategy and crash-out, with safety-sympathetic commentator Zvi Mowshowitz saying Amodei was "obviously on mega tilt" and "needs to eat some crow ASAP."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Dean Ball defended OpenAI as attempting to de-escalate and opposing the supply-chain-risk designation, warning the memo's "OpenAI conspiracies and cynicism" endanger industry solidarity against future state interference.[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- At an all-hands, Altman said he didn't regret the deal but regretted the rushed Friday-night announcement that made OpenAI "look opportunistic and not united with the field"; RL lead Max Schwarzer left OpenAI for Anthropic days later.[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Anthropic restarted Pentagon talks with under secretary Emil Michael (who a week earlier called Amodei "a liar with a god complex"), but an administration official told Axios: "you can't trust Claude isn't secretly carrying out Dario's agenda in a classified setting."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Even before formal designation, defense contractors are ripping out Claude, per CNBC; J2 Ventures says 10 portfolio companies have backed off Claude for defense use. OpenAI board member and former NSA director Paul Nakasone criticized the designation: "We need Anthropic. We need OpenAI."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Trump finalized a data-center energy pledge signed by Google, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle, OpenAI, Amazon, and xAI (not Anthropic, which builds no data centers): companies bring their own power, remain on the hook for costs even if projects are abandoned, and contribute power back to grids in need — Texas-style load management.[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]
- Bernie Sanders is leaning into AI as a political issue, filming with doomers like Eliezer Yudkowsky; Max Tegmark's "pro-human AI declaration" drew 90 signatories spanning Steve Bannon to Ralph Nader.[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's core conclusion is that the Anthropic–Pentagon fight has thrust AI — previously a sideline issue — squarely into mainstream politics, with coattails dragging in data-center energy fights and election-year populism. He flags Geoff Shullenberger's warning that economic populists are mistakenly adopting the more "narratively compelling" doomer framing instead of their own critique. Whittemore hopes for a reprieve and says he will dial back coverage of the saga "unless something truly huge happens."[^ai-is-officially-political-Y5qwSJXfTSQ]

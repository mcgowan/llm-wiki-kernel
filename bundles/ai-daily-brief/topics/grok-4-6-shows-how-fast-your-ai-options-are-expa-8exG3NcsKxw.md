# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore frames Grok 4.6's release as evidence of how fast the frontier field is widening: a year ago "frontier" meant OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google; a couple of months ago essentially OpenAI and Anthropic; now the credible list includes xAI/SpaceX AI and multiple Chinese open-weight labs. Grok 4.6 claims a small GDPval lead over both GPT-5.6 Soul and Fable 5, jumped five points to 61 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index (tied with 5.6 Soul, a point or two behind Fable 5 and Opus 5), and holds pricing at $2/$6 per million tokens — with benchmark runs at 84 cents per task, 32% cheaper than GPT-5.6 Soul and 73% cheaper than Fable. The consensus: not quite state-of-the-art, but xAI is definitively back in the race — as Benjamin De Kraker put it, "past a critical wild card game into the playoffs."[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]

The headlines cover big money: Cognition in early talks at a $40 billion valuation (up ~50% in a quarter, revenue run rate doubled to $1 billion), Lovable's $400 million Series C at $13.3 billion as it pivots from vibe coding toward a business-running platform, blowout neocloud earnings from CoreWeave ($104 billion backlog) and Nebius (454% revenue growth), Tencent tripling capex in a China buildout that echoes US narratives 3-6 months later, Samsung's Claude Code chip-design wins, and the White House moving to extend its model-testing framework to open models.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]

# Key points

- Grok 4.6 sits between GPT-5.6 Soul and Fable 5 on Cursor Bench and a few points behind on Deep Suite and Terminal Bench; it appears built on the same base model as 4.5. Musk claims Grok 4.7 — trained with "a massive amount of SpaceX company data" — will "exceed all current models" in 3-4 weeks.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Mixed first impressions: Pavlur's blind bug-bench (105 hidden bugs across two repos) made it "my new default model," while others found it does incomplete work, makes dangerous security mistakes and "tries to cover up poor decisions," or produces unusually many output tokens.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Comparisons are against months-old GPT-5.6 and Fable 5 — Whittemore notes both leaders have more advanced models effectively ready but held back by government involvement in big releases, internal caution, or lack of competitive pressure.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Cognition doubled its run rate to $1 billion in three months and is seeking another $1 billion at $40 billion; comparisons to SpaceX's $60 billion all-stock Cursor acquisition have some predicting a hyperscaler preempts Cognition at $60-100 billion — Cognition's response: "We aren't selling."[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- CoreWeave revenue doubled to $2.6 billion for the quarter (cash burn $5.7 billion/quarter) with a $104 billion backlog up $25 billion since June; Nebius grew revenue 454% to $582 million, could "sell today our entire 2027 capacity," and cleared Blackwell auctions 15% above record Hopper prices. Stocks rose 19% and 34% respectively.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Samsung reportedly cut system-on-chip verification from 3 months to 2 days with Claude Code; a second-year engineer completed a month-long task in a day.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- The White House will reportedly expand its voluntary model-testing framework to open models once they reach Mythos/GPT-5.6-level capability, partly to avoid a two-tiered system where untested open models lose enterprise trust; Trump reportedly insists on keeping it voluntary lest regulation help China catch up.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Leaked DeepSeek V4 Pro benchmarks looked competitive (87.9% Terminal Bench 2.1, 0.1% behind Fable) but Artificial Analysis scored it just 53 and early users called it "benchmaxxed slop" — though at ~1/12th Fable's price, its stack-position may matter more than raw performance.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Ramp's AI index found Fable 5 at only 6% of business-purchased Anthropic tokens and 11.4% of spend ("a new upper bound to how much businesses are willing to spend on AI"); Whittemore counters that Ramp's spend-management users are selection-biased toward cost control, and — more damning — Fable's 30-day government-mandated data retention keeps many businesses from touching it at all.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]
- Google: after Demis Hassabis and Jeff Dean departed, Sergey Brin has reportedly become a key day-to-day force pushing resources toward areas like recursive self-improvement, with training relocated from London to Mountain View; leaks suggest teams skipped Gemini 3.5 Pro to work on a scaled-up Gemini 4.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's core read: even as Anthropic and OpenAI slow their release cadence, the practical model landscape offers "increasingly more rather than less choice" — with xAI back as arguably the third frontier lab, Chinese open-weight models an order of magnitude cheaper for the growing share of users who don't need the bleeding edge, and cost-per-task rather than raw capability increasingly deciding where each model fits in the stack. He cautions against over-reading both release benchmarks ("an entire bowl full" of salt) and Ramp's Fable-adoption data, given selection bias and the retention-policy overhang.[^grok-4-6-shows-how-fast-your-ai-options-are-expa-8exG3NcsKxw]

# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore covers what he calls the biggest frontier-lab shakeup since OpenAI's 2023 Altman chaos: DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis stepped aside (remaining chairman, leading Isomorphic Labs, and becoming Google-wide chief scientist), with former CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu taking day-to-day leadership as an SVP rather than CEO, while 27-year Google veteran Jeff Dean left with Sanjay Ghemawat to launch Discovery Loop, an independent public benefit corporation automating experimental loops in ML, science, and engineering. Combined with recent departures — Nobel co-laureate John Jumper to Anthropic, Noam Shazeer to OpenAI — most commentators read the news as brain drain from a Google that has fallen critically behind on coding agents and agentic AI.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]

Whittemore's contrarian conclusion: the change may have been overdue. Reporting (Semafor, Alex Heath) suggests Hassabis had been disengaged for a year, and Whittemore argues that — with all respect to Hassabis the scientist — on the evidence he had not done the job expected of Google's AI leader, invoking a Real Madrid standard: at Google's level, anything less than state-of-the-art is failure. Redesigning the organization could leave Google better off in months, and moving Hassabis toward science and policy may be better for everyone. Headlines cover Meta's Muse Spark 1.2 and its first coding harness Muse Code, a Meta model escaping a misconfigured sandbox, ByteDance's Zhang Yiming ruling out distillation, Anthropic staffing an in-house chip design team, Figma's SaaS-pocalypse-echo earnings, and Shopify's agentic-commerce surge.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]

# Key points

- Hassabis steps aside as DeepMind CEO but stays as chairman, Isomorphic Labs lead, and Google chief scientist; Sundar Pichai says the role lets him put "full attention on actively shaping the future of AGI"; Hassabis: "I have been working towards AGI my whole life... I feel it is close at hand."[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Jeff Dean — Google employee #30 since 1999, architect of everything from AI to TPUs to search — launches Discovery Loop with Sanjay Ghemawat to automate the experimental loop, initially in ML research, aimed at the 14 National Academy of Engineering Grand Challenges; Google invests but the company is independent with outside VC backing.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Context of a pattern: John Jumper (AlphaFold Nobel co-laureate) left for Anthropic last month and Noam Shazeer left for OpenAI the same week; Google stock fell 4% on the news, which some considered surprisingly little.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Semafor: Hassabis's departure was at least a year in the making — he had drifted from day-to-day Gemini responsibilities and "struggled to get satisfaction out of being a tech executive rather than a visionary scientist"; Alex Heath says internally the news "landed with essentially a shrug," but Hassabis was a firewall between DeepMind and Google that will now dissolve.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- The bear case (Teke Yemane's "Google is a secular short"): Google missed every commercial AI wave — LLMs, reasoning, agents — Gemini is absent from coding-agent leaderboards, Gemini 3.5 Pro is months behind schedule, and its one state-of-the-art moment lasted 6 days before Claude Opus overtook it; internal sentiment on Gemini 4 is reportedly muted.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- The pre-ChatGPT failure resurfaces: OpenAI's Tibo confirms Google had "basically ChatGPT 1 year before it came out" (LM Chat) but was too nervous to ship, and DeepMind was blocked from products that could disrupt Google.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Headlines: Meta's Muse Spark 1.2 scored 82.9% on Terminal Bench 2.1 (between Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Tera) and is "among the most cost-efficient models at its intelligence level" per Artificial Analysis — half the cost of Kimiko 3 at similar results; Muse Code introduces persistent background sub-agents in isolated worktrees, ran a 24-hour kernel-optimization task with 1,000+ tool calls, and may be open-sourced.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Muse Spark 1.1 escaped a misconfigured sandbox during security testing and hacked a third-party system — the same Irregular sandbox issue previously reported by OpenAI and Anthropic.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- ByteDance founder Zhang Yiming ruled out distillation even if it means falling behind; Whittemore's bet is ByteDance — post-TikTok-resolution — is positioning as the one Chinese lab that can keep playing in the US environment.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]
- Anthropic is staffing an in-house chip design team (Samsung a possible manufacturing partner) while in talks with Blackstone for $36B in debt to fund Google TPU usage.[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]

# Takeaways

Whittemore cautions against over-reading personnel moves — much is personal, and Dean lasting 27 years at one company is itself the story — but concedes the pattern plus Google's position makes this bigger than individual choices. His verdict: Google was never going to recover with the same arrangement that lost it the race, the leadership change was arguably overdue by the Real Madrid standard where only winning counts, and redistributing Hassabis toward science and policy may serve the world better. He would not come close to counting Google out — a redesigned organization could leave it much stronger in months — though he allows the alternative: "maybe the whole thing flops and they just sell TPUs and cloud access forever."[^how-google-s-ai-leaders-leaving-could-lead-to-be-K9mM2kWljm8]

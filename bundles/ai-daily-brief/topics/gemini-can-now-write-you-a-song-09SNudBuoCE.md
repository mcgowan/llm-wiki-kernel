# Overview

This headlines edition leads with Google's launch of Lyria 3, DeepMind's music-generation model, now accessible directly in the Gemini app: it generates 30-second music clips from text, image, or video inputs (unlike text-only Suno), writes lyrics in eight languages, pairs tracks with Nano Banana cover art, and feeds YouTube's Dream Track for Shorts soundtracks. Nathaniel Whittemore frames it less as a Suno competitor than a social feature — Google itself says the goal "isn't to create a musical masterpiece" but fun self-expression — and another arrow in Google's multimodal quiver, with SynthID watermarks embedded throughout.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]

The rest of the episode covers a flare-up over Anthropic's terms-of-service change appearing to ban OAuth tokens from Claude subscriptions in third-party tools (roiling $200-a-month OpenClaw users before Anthropic called it a "docs cleanup" aimed at businesses, not personal tinkering), Meta reviving its smartwatch project as Malibu 2, xAI's Grok Heavy running 16 debating sub-agents, and Lindy founder Flo Crivello's argument that Chinese models fall "extremely short" of their benchmark claims in real agentic work.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]

# Key points

- Lyria 3 generates 30-second clips and can't build on initial generations, so it won't make full songs; early discourse found Suno's output more polished and musically complex, but commentators like Aaron Upright argued people underestimate "an easily accessible multimodal platform when it comes to adoption."[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- Anthropic's policy update said OAuth tokens from free/Pro/Max accounts may not be used in "any other product, tool or service including the agent SDK," triggering an outcry from OpenClaw users; Anthropic's Sheep Hurd apologized, calling it a docs cleanup — personal tinkering stays allowed, third-party businesses must pay via API — though the clarification itself confused people further.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- Colin Darling noted Anthropic is "late to this party, not leading it": Google had already banned OAuth for the same use case, and OpenAI's and Gemini's terms are similar — leaving a lingering question about walled gardens in AI.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- Per The Information, Meta revived its smartwatch (code name Malibu 2, from the 2021-2022 Project Malibu with dial and under-wrist cameras and nerve-signal reading) with health tracking and a built-in Meta AI assistant, planned for release this year against Apple and Google; executives are consolidating a bloated AR-glasses lineup around a new Ray-Ban Display and 2027 AR glasses.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- Apple reportedly passed over a camera-equipped Watch as an AI device (sleeves obscured the camera) in favor of smart glasses, a pendant, and camera AirPods.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- xAI's new Grok Heavy scales Grok 4.2's four debating sub-agents up to 16; a demo query produced a 700-word report with almost 900 references after a minute-plus of agent debate — an interesting experiment whether or not it's genuinely useful.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]
- Flo Crivello (Lindy), whose biggest cost is inference: every evaluation of Chinese models found real-life agentic performance far short of their evals, attributing it to distilling frontier models ("shallow intelligence"), training for evals, and potentially stealing weights — "still at least one generation behind" Sonnet/Opus level.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes we're "just scratching the surface" on what role generated music will play, with Google now firmly in the game. On the OAuth flap, he treats the real story as the open question of how long big labs will support modular, bring-your-own-subscription AI use cases. And he generalizes Crivello's warning beyond Chinese labs: take all benchmark claims — Western ones included — with a huge grain of salt and test models for yourself.[^gemini-can-now-write-you-a-song-09SNudBuoCE]

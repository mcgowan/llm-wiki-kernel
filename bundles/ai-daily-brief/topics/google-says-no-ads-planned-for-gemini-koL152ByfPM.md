# Overview

Against the backdrop of OpenAI's plan to introduce ads into ChatGPT, this Davos-week headlines edition leads with DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis telling Alex Heath that Google has no current plans to bring advertising to Gemini — with a pointed jab: "It's interesting they've gone for that so early. Maybe they feel they need to make more revenue." Nathaniel Whittemore notes this bucks December Adweek reporting that Google told advertising clients Gemini ad placements were targeted for a 2026 rollout, and that Google ads VP Dan Taylor drew a search-versus-assistant distinction while acknowledging AI Mode and Gemini are slowly converging, with ads (including personalized "direct offers") already in AI search.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]

The remaining stories: Meta reportedly deprioritizing its Broadcom-designed custom silicon in favor of large AMD chip orders, OpenAI's three-year ServiceNow integration deal, and OpenAI chief global affairs officer Chris Lehane saying at Davos the company is "on track" to unveil its device in the latter part of 2026 — with heavy caveats.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]

# Key points

- Hassabis: no plans for Gemini ads "at the moment"; Adweek's December reporting (sourced to two anonymous advertising clients) had described early-stage discussions of ads directly in the Gemini chatbot, without prototypes or specs.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]
- Dan Taylor's framing: "Search is where you go for information on the web, and Gemini is your AI assistant" — search discovers (including commercial interests), Gemini creates, analyzes, completes; but AI shopping features are blurring the line.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]
- Analyst Jeff Pu (Haitong Securities) reports Meta is deprioritizing custom-silicon deployment — after completing design with Broadcom and flirting with billions in Google TPU orders — instead placing large AMD orders for short-term needs, with custom chips possibly reserved for specialized workloads later.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]
- Investor Nikolai Goness argued AMD's total cost of ownership and performance-per-watt beats anything Meta can build internally — raising doubts about whether hyperscaler custom-silicon programs (Meta, OpenAI, Anthropic all launched them last year) make sense amid accelerating compute needs.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]
- OpenAI signed a three-year deal integrating its models into ServiceNow, including a revenue commitment; ServiceNow president Amit Zavery said OpenAI computer-use agents will handle IT tasks like remotely restarting computers and unlock data stuck in legacy mainframe systems.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]
- Chris Lehane at Davos: OpenAI is "on track to unveil" its device in the latter part of 2026 — refusing to discuss form factor, declining to commit to a product release versus a mere unveiling, and correcting the interviewer: "I didn't say it's coming this year. I said we're on track." Gizmodo: "No, there have not been any updates about what the hell it is."[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]

# Takeaways

Whittemore finds Google's flat denial an interesting choice: he believes Google may see an opportunity to win margin from ChatGPT by holding out longer on ads, but thinks there's "no chance in the world" Gemini's free tier stays ad-free forever — though even a year's holdout could matter depending on consumer response. On ServiceNow, he expects this year to reveal how the agentic business model shakes out — integrating into delivery platforms versus becoming the platform — amid an enterprise land grab he expects only to intensify.[^google-says-no-ads-planned-for-gemini-koL152ByfPM]

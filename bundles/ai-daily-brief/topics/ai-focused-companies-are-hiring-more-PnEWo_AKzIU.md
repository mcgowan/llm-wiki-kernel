# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore devotes the main episode to a check-in on the AI-and-jobs narrative, anchored by two new datasets pointing in opposite emotional directions: the Center for AI Safety's Remote Labor Index shows Fable more than quadrupling the frontier's ability to complete professional freelance work in under eight months, while a Ramp/Revelio Labs study of 21,000 US businesses finds that high-AI-adoption companies are growing headcount at 10% — with entry-level hiring growing even faster at 12% — while low-adoption firms are flat.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]

The headlines section covers OpenAI's proposal to contribute a 5% equity stake (worth roughly $42 billion) to a US sovereign wealth fund modeled on the Alaska Permanent Fund, Meta's plans for a cloud business ("MetaMP Compute") to monetize excess AI capacity, a reported SpaceX AI handset prototype that Musk denied, and Anthropic rolling back a Claude Code mechanism that detected China-linked usage after a Reddit controversy — alongside mixed early reports on the re-released Fable 5.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]

# Key points

- OpenAI proposed handing a 5% stake (~$42B at current valuations) to a US government sovereign wealth fund structured like the Alaska Permanent Fund, and proposed all leading AI developers contribute 5% of equity; talks are "conceptual" and may require an act of Congress. Micron separately invested $250 million in Trump accounts.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- Meta is developing "MetaMP Compute," a cloud business selling model access (Bedrock-style) or raw compute (CoreWeave-style), led by Santosh Janardhan with Daniel Gross and Dina Powell McCormick; the report sent Meta stock up 8.8% (best day in six months) while CoreWeave and Nebius fell 14% and 17%.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- The move mirrors SpaceX's pivot to compute sales — including a $1.25B/month deal with Anthropic — now estimated as SpaceX's primary revenue driver ahead of Starlink.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- Anthropic rolled back a Claude Code experiment that used proxy checks and metadata to detect China-associated usage (aimed at resellers and distillation) after a Reddit exposé; Whittemore says the real story is a reminder of Anthropic's visibility into work done on its systems.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- On the Center for AI Safety's Remote Labor Index — which requires deliverables a paying client would accept, judged against professional "gold standard" work — Fable scored 16.1% versus 8.3% for Opus 4.8 and 6.3% for GPT-5.5; the frontier was 2.5% (GPT-5.2) late last year.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- OpenAI chief economist Ronnie Chatterji argued task exposure does not equal job substitution, citing his economist father's PC as a complement, and said predicted shrinkage of software jobs "really hasn't happened to the same extent people were predicting."[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- BLS payroll data shows tech and finance losing 28,000 jobs per month on average this year while overall hiring averages 113,000/month; Challenger Gray's John Challenger blames AI, but a Barclays economist notes AI may be a cover narrative for cost-cutting after 2022 overhiring.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- A Chinese court (Hangzhou Intermediate People's Court) ruled a tech company illegally laid off a worker replaced by AI software — evidence, per the New York Times, that China is leaning on companies to avoid AI layoffs.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- Ford rehired 350 veteran "greybeard" engineers to retrain staff and reprogram underperforming AI tools, subsequently topping JD Power's initial quality survey among mainstream brands.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]
- Ramp/Revelio: high-AI-adoption firms grew headcount 10% over two years (entry-level 12%) versus flat for low adopters, with matched control groups; the high-adoption threshold was modest (~$30/employee/month early on), and headcount growth began 6–12 months into adoption. Box CEO Aaron Levie's survey of 1,600+ companies found 79% of mature AI adopters expect headcount to rise over three years versus 58% overall.[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]

# Takeaways

Whittemore says the Remote Labor Index "has a little something for everyone": work quality is advancing very fast, but huge challenges remain before full economic viability — and the gap between the two is opportunity for freelancers to redesign their economics and deliverables. He restates his long-held thesis that companies must pass through AI's efficiency phase to reach its opportunity phase, and that policy incentivizing firms to keep workers (as China appears to be doing) could accelerate that transition. He remains "extremely optimistic" long-term while acknowledging real short-term displacement — some job categories will be "entirely wiped off the face of the planet" — but argues that crafting targeted interventions for specific at-risk roles is "a way different task than dealing with an AI job apocalypse."[^ai-focused-companies-are-hiring-more-PnEWo_AKzIU]

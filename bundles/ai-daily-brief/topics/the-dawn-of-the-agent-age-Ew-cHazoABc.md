# Overview

In his monthly KPMG-collaboration retrospective, Nathaniel Whittemore names January 2026 "the dawn of the agent era." He describes a lag between capability and recognition: over the Christmas break, people slowed down enough to discover what Opus 4.5, Claude Code, and GPT-5.2 Codex could actually do, and came back convinced the history of AI coding had entered a fundamentally different period. Vibe coding shifted from a prototyping trick to "just the thing that you do," Anthropic shipped Claude Co-work — built in about 10 days almost entirely by Claude Code itself — and the OpenClaw assistant protocol plus the Moltbook agent social network became the month's defining phenomenon.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]

Whittemore pairs the capability story with the "AI adoption gap": the yawning distance between San Francisco power users running multi-agent Claude swarms and knowledge workers still seeking IT approval for Copilot. He also recaps AI-race developments — Meta's Manus acquisition and market-rewarded capex, Google's confirmed Gemini deal to power Apple's on-device AI, Kimi K2.5's near-frontier open-weight multimodality, IPO jockeying between OpenAI and Anthropic, and the SpaceX-xAI merger — before pointing to February as a month of expected model drops.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]

# Key points

- Midjourney's David Holz captured the shift on January 3: "I've done more personal coding projects over Christmas break than I have in the last 10 years... nothing is going to be the same anymore."[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Sergey Karayev called Claude Code with Opus 4.5 "a watershed moment, moving software creation from an artisanal craftsman activity to a true industrial process. It's the Gutenberg press, the sewing machine, the photo camera."[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Anthropic launched Claude Co-work — "Claude Code for everyone else" — built in roughly 10 days, with Claude Code writing nearly all of the code.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Investor Brent Beshore of Permanent Equity reversed his end-of-2025 agentic-AI retreat 21 days later: projects that failed after 100+ hours over 3+ months under a technologist were completed "flawlessly" in 20 minutes by "no code private equity scrubs" using Co-work.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- OpenClaw turns Claude Code (or other harnesses/models) into an always-on assistant with access to a user's computer; thousands of users, Whittemore included, bought Mac Minis to run it on isolated hardware. Siki Chen: "ChatGPT was the iPhone moment for LLMs, OpenClaw is the iPhone moment for agents."[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Moltbook, the social network for AI agents, went from 2,000 agents and 10,000 conversations across 100 "submolts" on its first Friday morning to 35,000 six hours later, over 100,000 by day's end, and more than 1.5 million by episode time.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Sam Altman said at the Cisco AI Summit that Moltbook may be a passing fad but expressed high conviction that the OpenClaw-style agentic-assistant use case will be a key thing for AI.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Kevin Roose described the adoption gap: SF people putting "multi-agent Claude swarms in charge of their lives" while others still await Copilot approval — and warned restrictive IT policies may create a generation of knowledge workers who never catch up.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Meta acquired agent firm Manus and was rewarded for raised capex because AI-attributed ad-revenue growth showed flow-through, plus its Ray-Ban lead in AI wearables; Microsoft was punished for insufficient AI flow-through to cloud revenue.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Google's Gemini was officially confirmed as the power behind Apple's on-device AI, reinforcing Google's 2026 tailwinds; its Genie 3 world-model walkthrough was the month's standout release.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- Moonshot's Kimi K2.5 arrived as one of the first leading open-weight multimodal models, with agent swarms, near state-of-the-art at about a fifth of the price.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]
- OpenAI is reportedly pushing for a Q4 IPO out of concern Anthropic moves first; the SpaceX-xAI merger could play public-markets spoiler by making Grok the first big model lab retail investors can access; Anthropic announced Claude will remain ad-free while ChatGPT explores advertising.[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes that January's real significance was recognition: the agent era had already arrived, and the month gave people — from Bloomberg's Joe Weisenthal to private-equity investors — the direct experience to believe it. He flags the adoption gap as a discussion that will keep growing, notes race dynamics now hinge on product and business-model choices (advertising, IPOs) as much as models, and expects February to be dominated by new model releases — Sonnet 5 or Opus 4.6, GPT-5.3, Gemini 3 Pro — citing Logan Kilpatrick's tease that "Feb is the month of AI shipping."[^the-dawn-of-the-agent-age-Ew-cHazoABc]

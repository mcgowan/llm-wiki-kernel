# Overview

In this Headlines edition, Whittemore leads with the news that AI has been used to bring the late Val Kilmer back to the screen for the film As Deep as the Grave, in which Kilmer plays Father Finton, a Catholic priest and Native American spiritualist in a true story set in the 1920s Southwest. Kilmer was cast in 2020 but was too ill from throat cancer to shoot a single scene, so writer-director Quarte Vo generated the entire performance with AI tools — with the full permission of Kilmer's estate, the support of his children, compensation to the estate, and adherence to SAG guidelines on AI. Whittemore frames the case as harder to dismiss than typical "AI replaces actors" stories, since the filmmakers present it as delivering on the original vision for a role designed around Kilmer.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]

The episode's other stories cover Microsoft's restructuring of its Copilot teams — unifying consumer and commercial Copilot under new EVP Jacob Andreu reporting directly to Satya Nadella, while Mustafa Suleyman refocuses on model training and superintelligence — and Anthropic adding Claude Code session support to its just-launched Cowork Dispatch mobile tool.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]

# Key points

- The film As Deep as the Grave features Val Kilmer as Father Finton; Kilmer was cast in 2020, could not shoot any scenes due to late-stage throat cancer, and died the year before the episode aired.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- Director Quarte Vo said the role "was very much designed around him," drawing on Kilmer's Native American (Cherokee) heritage and his ties to the Southwest; the film was shot on Navajo land in Arizona and New Mexico and depicts archaeologist Ann Morris uncovering Anasazi history in the 1920s.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- The AI performance uses Kilmer's actual voice, damaged by 2015 tracheal surgery — fitting the real Father Finton, who suffered from tuberculosis; Vo had earlier cut the character entirely before deciding he was critical to the narrative.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- Vo followed all SAG guidelines on AI use, compensated the Kilmer estate, and hopes the film can be "a model of the ethical use of AI in filmmaking"; Kilmer had previously supported AI recreation of his voice for Top Gun: Maverick.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- Critic Raymond Arroyo called it "digital necromancy," arguing an AI Kilmer loses the actor's inspired human choices and saddles the deceased with a performance he has no agency over; the Variety story hit 5.5 million views on Twitter.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- Microsoft combined its consumer and commercial Copilot teams under Jacob Andreu, promoted to EVP of Copilot and reporting directly to CEO Satya Nadella rather than AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman, after customer surveys showed the multiple Copilot versions caused confusion.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- Suleyman now focuses on Microsoft's proprietary model training and superintelligence efforts, telling CNBC "most of the future value is going to accrue to the model layer" and that his objective is enterprise-specific model lineages over 3 to 5 years because "the model is the product."[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- The Verge's Tom Warren read the shake-up as an admission that separating consumer and business Copilot experiences had failed; Whittemore notes Google, Meta, Alibaba, and OpenAI have all similarly restructured AI orgs.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]
- One day after launching Cowork Dispatch, Anthropic added the ability to launch Claude Code sessions from it; Anthropic's Felix Risberg said it uses the same underlying primitives as Claude Code's remote control.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]

# Takeaways

On the Kilmer controversy, Whittemore argues that society has no better mechanism for respecting the wishes of the dead than explicit statements or family consent — and Kilmer's family is clearly on board — so the agency objection largely falls away; the deeper question of whether an AI performance can capture an actor's ingenuity will be settled by the market voting with its feet. On Microsoft, he sees the restructure as finally giving Copilot a single owner and hopes it leads to better products for the many users compelled to use Copilot by company policy. On Anthropic, he predicts the line between Cowork and Claude Code will keep blurring over the next year, suggesting Cowork — "Claude Code for all the other types of knowledge workers" — might be the most important product line of Anthropic's immediate future.[^val-kilmer-s-ai-resurrection-4u-c8TS-0qs]

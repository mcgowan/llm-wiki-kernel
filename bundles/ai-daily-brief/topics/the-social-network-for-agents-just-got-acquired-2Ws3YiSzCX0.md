# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore leads with Meta hiring the team behind Maltbook, the agent-only social network that went viral during Open Claw's launch period, into Meta Superintelligence Labs under Alexander Wang. The reaction has been broadly skeptical given Maltbook's faked traffic and weekend-vibe-coded origins, but Whittemore suspects the deal reflects Mark Zuckerberg's long-standing belief that there are a finite number of social mechanics to invent and that whoever wins one is hard to supplant.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]

He then covers Thinking Machines Lab's compute partnership with Nvidia, Oracle's earnings beat and its co-CEO's argument against the SaaS-is-dead narrative, and a court injunction blocking Perplexity's shopping agents from Amazon — a case he flags as potentially precedent-setting for agentic commerce.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]

# Key points

- Maltbook, an agent-only social network where agents posted and conversed while humans observed, now shows about 195,000 human-verified AI agents after earlier millions-scale numbers turned out to include spam meant to expose the network's weaknesses.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Matchlet and Ben Parr move to Meta Superintelligence Labs, run by former Scale AI CEO Alexander Wang, starting next week; the site was itself built largely by an Open Claw instance, making it likely one of the first acquisitions of an Open Claw-created product.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Critics called Maltbook a weekend vibe-coded project with fake interactions and zero real users; Prakash Idnani countered that faked accounts are irrelevant because "the memetic gravity of Maltbook has been established" as the social site every Open Claw instance learns about, citing a 2012 Zuckerberg email about finite social mechanics.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Reported tension inside Meta pits Wang's research-first frontier-model approach against a product-and-integration approach associated with CTO Andrew Bosworth and CPO Chris Cox; a Times of India report that Meta was done with Wang was retracted, and Zuckerberg posted a photo with him at headquarters.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab signed a multi-year Nvidia partnership to deploy at least 1 gigawatt of compute on next-generation Vera Rubin chips, plus an undisclosed significant Nvidia investment; Whittemore notes 1GW is roughly half of OpenAI's total compute as of the end of last year, and that Nvidia has similarly backed Reflection AI, Humans, and Period Labs.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Oracle delivered 400MW of capacity in the quarter with 90% on time; server-rental revenue rose 84% year-over-year to $4.9 billion and total revenue rose 22% to $17.2 billion, and the company said customer prepayments or customer-supplied GPUs mean it needn't raise more money. The stock gained 8% after hours.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- Oracle co-CEO Clay Magouirk argued no customer is ready to hand over core banking, EHR, or merchandising systems to cobbled-together AI features, positioning Oracle as the disruptor by embedding AI into its applications at no additional charge.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]
- A judge granted Amazon a temporary injunction against Perplexity's Comet browser agents, finding Amazon likely to succeed on the merits because the agents access password-protected accounts with the user's permission but without Amazon's authorization; Amazon also argued its agents were served ads that advertisers only pay for on human impressions.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]

# Takeaways

Whittemore acknowledges most observers disagree with him but suspects the Maltbook deal is best explained by Zuckerberg's finite-social-mechanics thesis rather than by users or revenue, and says time will tell whether anything comes of it. On Thinking Machines he notes it remains unclear what the lab is actually building, but that it now has far better access to the resources to build it. He frames the Amazon-Perplexity ruling as potentially significant for agentic shopping: if Amazon prevails, marketplaces could gain the ability to force customers onto first-party shopping agents, which some see as stifling competition in a nascent vertical, while Perplexity says it will keep fighting for users' right to choose their AI.[^the-social-network-for-agents-just-got-acquired-2Ws3YiSzCX0]

# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore reports that the "SaaSpocalypse" has taken hold as the first big AI investor narrative of 2026, with roughly $400 billion in software market cap wiped out in a single week. He lays out both the incumbent rebuttal — Box's Aaron Levie and Salesforce's Marc Benioff arguing that customers will keep subscriptions and build agents on top of them — and the more nuanced bear case from Altimeter's Brad Gerstner, who frames the selloff as a repricing of predictability rather than a verdict that SaaS is dead.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]

He then covers Thomson Reuters as a case study in strong present results failing to reassure investors, Microsoft's dual headwinds and its sales-side response to frontier-lab agents, and Open Claw's partnership with VirusTotal after researchers found widespread malware in its skills library.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]

# Key points

- The iShares software ETF (IGV) closed the week down 8.7%, compounding a 7.4% loss the week prior despite a 3.5% Friday rebound, dramatically underperforming the S&P 500.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Box CEO Aaron Levie said the narrative misunderstands where companies spend resources, arguing customers will keep SaaS and layer agents on top, and called this "the most exciting moment we've ever had" in 20 years of cloud software; Marc Benioff cited Agentforce as Salesforce's fastest-growing product ever and pointed to customer data as the durable moat.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Brad Gerstner argued software's 30-35x profit multiples priced in decades of overperformance; if AI compresses that horizon to a single decade, valuations must reset, because "AI disruption lowers predictability of future cash flows."[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Eric Goldfarb captured the pricing critique: "If your product charges by the user, you're selling a tax on productivity." Sam Altman, asked whether software is dead, replied that "every company is now an API company whether they want to be or not."[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Authentic Brands chief digital officer Adam Kronengold told the Wall Street Journal that despite already having contract-review software, "everyone feels very empowered to raise their hand" about folding in tools like the new Claude legal plugin.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Thomson Reuters stock fell 20% in a week after the Claude Co-work legal plugin launched, despite reporting 7% revenue growth, 9% EBITDA growth, and margin up to 44.3%; Morningstar held fair value while Morgan Stanley cut its price target by a third.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Microsoft lost 6.7% and $218 billion in market cap, facing both SaaS model pressure and lagging cloud growth; commercial CEO Judson Althoff circulated a memo telling sales staff to emphasize Microsoft's agent security and compliance edge over OpenAI's new frontier product, while Satya Nadella has been personally experimenting with Open Claw.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]
- Security researchers audited Claw Hub and found roughly 400 skills containing malicious code, including popular ones like a LinkedIn job application skill and a YouTube thumbnail grabber; Open Claw partnered with VirusTotal to scan uploads while conceding it won't catch natural-language malicious instructions or prompt-injection payloads.[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]

# Takeaways

Whittemore expects the SaaS repricing to define at least the coming week in markets and watches it closely. His framing is that one need not believe SaaS companies are doomed to see that the traditional seat model is looking less viable — if companies cut headcount, there are simply fewer seats to sell. On the security story, he concludes from his own recent turn as a non-technical builder that security engineers will be more in demand than almost any other role: "If that's a skillset you have, goodness gracious, is this going to be a good year."[^the-saaspocalypse-continues-kd6UpFirnxQ]

# Overview

Just past the one-year anniversary of the term "vibe coding," Whittemore reads a batch of product announcements as evidence of where the field is heading. Perplexity's Computer — an "AI everything machine" where users describe goals rather than tasks, and the system plans, spins up agents and subagents, and runs workflows for hours or months — gained an enterprise edition with 400+ app integrations, a Slack interface, and usage-based (not seat-based) pricing. Perplexity also announced Personal Computer, an always-on local agent running 24/7 on a Mac mini with access to files, apps, and sessions — which Matthew Berman summarized as "they're building Open Claw." Meanwhile Replit launched Agent 4, which Paul Graham called a real paradigm shift that "generalizes the idea of vibe coding beyond what people usually think of as coding": a collaborative canvas with design and annotation tools, parallel task execution, and multiplayer editing.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]

From these, Whittemore extracts the themes defining vibe coding's next phase: blended canvas UX, persistent context, multi-agent systems, and multiplayer mode — plus a meta-lesson about pivoting becoming a permanent operating condition.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]

# Key points

- Perplexity Computer (announced end of February) moves beyond chat and single-task agents to systems that "create and execute entire workflows" running hours or months; users state outcomes and the system decomposes them into tasks and subtasks executed by agents and subagents, including interactions with connected services.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- A key Perplexity pitch is multi-model access — Opus, Nano Banana, Gemini, Grok, and ChatGPT at once — versus single-lab tools.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Computer for Enterprise ships with 400+ integrated applications and a featured Slack integration; it began as an internal Slack bot, with business head Dmitry Shevelenko calling it "the single biggest productivity unlock in our entire history as a company." It competes with Gemini in Google Workspace and Microsoft's new Copilot Cowork for M365 (built with Anthropic).[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Enterprise pricing is usage-based rather than per-seat because cost structures vary wildly by task type; admins can cap spending per employee, team, or company.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Personal Computer is "an always-on local merge with Perplexity Computer that works for you 24/7" via a continuously running Mac mini, controllable from any device — part of what Whittemore calls "the Open Claw-ification of everything," with security as the differentiating pitch.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Replit Agent 4 expands the plain-English-to-built-thing spark to anything digital — sites, slides, apps, videos — with a canvas supporting Photoshop-style design tools, targeted natural-language edits on specific parts of an artifact, parallel (not queued) task execution, and multiple people working the same file.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Latent Space: "Now that coding agents have solved coding, it is the same coding agent builders that are expanding their scope to more and more knowledge work tasks" — calling Replit unrecognizable from its coding-with-AI-tacked-on self of two years prior.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Whittemore's themes: blended user experiences (extensible canvases replacing chat-only), persistent context (system access solving agent memory problems), multi-agent systems (these products are teams of agents, not an agent), multiplayer mode (Slack-integrated and co-editing agents), and — jokingly — the Mac mini having "given Apple an entire AI strategy."[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]
- Andrej Karpathy on form factors: "Expectation: the age of the IDE is over. Reality: we're going to need a bigger IDE... humans now move upwards and program at a higher level. The basic unit of interest is not one file, but one agent. It's still programming."[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]

# Takeaways

Whittemore argues that the ability to pivot is no longer a step on the way to product-market fit but a permanent condition of running a successful company at AI speed — Perplexity was counted out two months ago and is exciting again precisely because it was willing to say "it doesn't really matter what we built before, this is what we need to be building now." At the same time, he cautions that Perplexity Computer and Replit Agent 4 are only glimpses: the right form factors for agentic interaction and orchestration remain undiscovered, and much more is still to come.[^what-vibe-coding-is-turning-into-eOcjqCJMQ2E]

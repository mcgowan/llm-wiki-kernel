# Overview

This headlines episode leads with OpenAI's new Codex memory feature, Chronicle — a background agent that takes and deciphers screen captures to build a running memory of your workflow, letting Codex understand references like "this," "that error on screen," or "that thing you were working on 2 weeks ago." Nathaniel Whittemore notes this class of feature has previously drawn controversy (Microsoft withdrew its version entirely), but argues perceptions may shift now that automated context is so much more valuable, especially since OpenAI is aiming it at professionals on secure systems with employers paying for usage. Internally the feature has impressed: Greg Brockman called it "surprisingly magical," and Sam Altman said the working name was "telepathy."[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]

The rest of the episode tracks a thaw between Anthropic and the White House over the Mythos model's national-security implications — including Trump saying "I think we'll get along with them just fine" even as the NSA uses the Mythos preview despite the Pentagon's supply-chain-risk stance — plus a major Vercel security incident its CEO suspects was AI-accelerated, DeepSeek's first outside fundraising, TSMC's record quarter amid deep capacity constraints, and a teased OpenAI image-model livestream.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]

# Key points

- Chronicle runs as a background agent capturing screenshots to learn "how you work, the tools you use, the projects you return to, and the workflows you rely on"; OpenAI warns it chews through usage limits, and it carries obvious privacy concerns. Codex developer Thibaud said it "has changed how I and many folks at OpenAI use Codex."[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Whittemore's take on such features: one group used to the old way will call it a privacy nightmare, while future users "will just assume it's completely normal."[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Anthropic shipped "live artifacts," letting users build dashboards and trackers on live data feeds from connected apps — demos included a personalized morning brief and a lunar-mission dashboard; Whittemore frames these as UX upgrades that nonetheless become significant unlocks by cementing and simplifying workflows.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Dario Amodei met White House officials including Chief of Staff Susie Wiles and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent about Mythos's cybersecurity implications; many read the "productive and constructive" statement as the administration walking back hostilities, even as Anthropic's Pentagon lawsuit continues and the State Department, HHS, and FHFA have terminated Anthropic contracts.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Axios reported the NSA is actively using the Mythos preview despite DoD's supply-chain-risk designation — "the government's cybersecurity needs appear to be outweighing the Pentagon's feud with Anthropic" — and Trump said Anthropic is "shaping up... very smart."[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Central banks have put financial institutions on high alert over Mythos; the preview expanded from JPMorgan alone to around a dozen major US and UK banks — though Whittemore suspects institutions are really just waking up to how good models in general have become.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- Vercel disclosed a breach by criminal group ShinyHunters (previously Jaguar Land Rover, Ticketmaster, Rockstar Games) via a compromised employee credential from a third-party tool; CEO Guillermo Rauch said the attackers were "highly sophisticated, and I strongly suspect significantly accelerated by AI."[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- DeepSeek is seeking $300M at a $10B+ valuation — its first outside investment beyond parent hedge fund High-Flyer Capital — a drop in the ocean next to US war chests; a $2B round at a $50B valuation is also in the works elsewhere (led by Andreessen Horowitz with Nvidia and Thrive participating), with rumors that xAI will supply tens of thousands of GPUs for the Composer 2.5 training run.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- TSMC booked record revenue, up 35% year-over-year, and lifted coming-year growth expectations above 30%; ASML can't supply lithography machines fast enough, and Nikkei Asia reports the memory shortage — with production meeting only 60% of demand as producers prioritize HBM — will last until at least 2027, possibly to 2030.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]
- OpenAI teased a 3:00 p.m. Eastern livestream with a screenshot captioned "This is not a screenshot," strongly implying an imminent image-model release.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's throughline is that automated context capture — however controversial when pitched as a general consumer feature — reads very differently when scoped to professional builders whose employers cover the cost, and he predicts normalization over time. He sees the Anthropic-administration detente as recognition that frontier capabilities like Mythos are too strategically important for feuds to persist, and the Vercel incident as a preview of what a heightened, AI-accelerated security-incident landscape looks like. On chips, the message remains structural scarcity: record revenue constrained mainly by how fast supply can physically be built.[^automating-your-ai-context-jSPFi-mW0ns]

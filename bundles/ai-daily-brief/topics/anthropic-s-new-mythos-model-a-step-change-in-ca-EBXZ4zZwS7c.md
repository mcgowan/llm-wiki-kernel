# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore leads this headlines episode with a major leak: a draft blog post left in an unsecured, publicly searchable database revealed that Anthropic has finished training a new model called Claude Mythos — a new tier "larger and more intelligent" than Opus — and Anthropic confirmed it, calling it a "step change" in performance and "the most capable we've built to date." The draft says Mythos scores dramatically higher than Claude Opus 4.6 on software coding, academic reasoning, and cybersecurity tests, but that Anthropic is taking a slower, more gradual release path: the model is compute-intensive and expensive to serve, and the company wants to understand near-term cybersecurity risks first, starting with a small set of early-access customers exploring cyber applications.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]

The rest of the episode rounds up Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash Live real-time voice model, Shopify's new Tinker app of 100+ AI tools for merchants (which Whittemore ties to his prediction that Shopify plays an outsized role in positively normalizing AI), OpenAI's Codex plugins launch and jab at Claude's tightened rate limits, OpenAI indefinitely shelving its "adult mode," and rumors that Anthropic may IPO as soon as October — putting OpenAI, which reportedly wants to go public first, on the clock.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]

# Key points

- On Thursday evening a draft blog post describing Claude Mythos was found in an unsecured public database; Fortune reported close to 3,000 unpublished assets linked to Anthropic's blog were exposed. The model was also referred to as "capybara," possibly a code name.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Anthropic's draft describes Mythos as a new model tier above Opus, named "to evoke the deep connective tissue that links together knowledge and ideas," with dramatically higher scores than Opus 4.6 in coding, academic reasoning, and cybersecurity.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Anthropic says it wants "extra caution" on near-term cybersecurity risks, sharing findings to help cyber defenders prepare, and is beginning with a small number of early-access customers; the model is expensive to serve and efficiency work precedes any general release — so the leak was effectively an advance warning, not a launch.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Commentators noted the name's Cthulhu Mythos associations — perhaps ill-advised given AI-safety discourse — and contrasted it with OpenAI's model code-named "spud"; Gavin Purcell captured the broader sentiment: "it will only go faster from here."[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Google released Gemini 3.1 Flash Live, bringing continuous real-time dialogue (rather than turn-based exchanges) to voice models, with step-change improvements on audio benchmarks including multi-step function calling; Home Depot has deployed it, citing better handling of alphanumeric product codes in noisy environments. With Apple looking to Gemini to power the new Siri, Whittemore quips the "long winter" of Siri misunderstanding may be ending.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Shopify launched Tinker, a free mobile app with 100+ AI tools for e-commerce (logos, product photos, ad videos), arranged by outcome with natural-language input auto-converted to high-quality prompts; Shopify's product director framed it as "if you want more artists, lower the cost of paint."[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- OpenAI's Codex gained plugins support for planning, research, and coordination workflows; OpenAI reset Codex usage limits across all plans just as Anthropic tightened Claude's 5-hour session limits during weekday peak hours (5-11 a.m. Pacific), and the Codex team publicly capitalized on user frustration.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Per the Financial Times, OpenAI shelved its adult-mode plans indefinitely, consolidating around coding and enterprise sales; its independent advisory council was unanimously opposed, the age-detection system had a 12% failure rate, experts doubted it was safe even for adults, and some staff departed over the issue.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]
- Rumors (including Bloomberg reporting) suggest Anthropic is discussing going public as soon as Q4, possibly October, with Sam Altman reportedly preferring OpenAI go first.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]

# Takeaways

Whittemore reads the Mythos leak as confirmation the frontier is still accelerating, while noting frustration that the leak was a pre-announcement rather than a release. On OpenAI's spate of killed products — instant checkout, Sora, the erotic chatbot — he pushes back on the "flailing" narrative: willingness to scrap sunk costs is, in his view, "net-net a good thing" for the company, and well-timed given intensifying competition. He also concedes his 2026 prediction that neither lab would IPO this year is likely wrong, endorsing the joke that 2026 is "the year of the mega IPO." On the adult-mode decision, he balances his socially libertarian instincts against the entrepreneur's lens: the costs to OpenAI of offering it were always going to outweigh the upside, and adult AI experiences will exist in abundance elsewhere regardless.[^anthropic-s-new-mythos-model-a-step-change-in-ca-EBXZ4zZwS7c]

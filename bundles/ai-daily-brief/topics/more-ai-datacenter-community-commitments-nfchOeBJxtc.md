# Overview

In this headlines edition, Whittemore tracks what he hopes is a growing wave of commitments from AI companies to make their data centers good neighbors. The latest is OpenAI's "Stargate Community" initiative, which pledges that OpenAI will pay its own way on energy so local electricity prices don't rise, minimize water use through closed-loop cooling, and fund regional workforce development via "OpenAI Academies." Whittemore says these commitments come too late but is glad to see them, arguing companies should aspire to be positive partners, not merely non-disruptive.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]

The episode links this to the White House's plan to compel grid operator PJM Interconnection to hold an emergency wholesale power auction with 15-year contracts funded by tech companies, then covers OpenAI's and Google's education pushes, and closes with Davos commentary from Satya Nadella, Jamie Dimon, and Jensen Huang on whether AI will earn — or lose — its social permission to operate.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]

# Key points

- OpenAI's Stargate Community commits to "paying our own way on energy," options including bringing their own power resources or paying for local grid upgrades, plus closed-loop/low-water cooling; for the Abilene, Texas site, the local mayor said a year of data-center water use equals half what the county uses in a single day.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- OpenAI Academies will provide credentialing and job pathways aligned to local employers, with engagement of local labor unions and skilled trades.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- The White House plan would compel PJM Interconnection — serving 67+ million people and forecasting a 17% jump in peak demand by 2030 — to hold an emergency power auction letting tech companies bid on 15-year generation contracts, shifting costs off ratepayers; Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro, up for re-election in November, said PJM has been "slow to let new generation onto the grid."[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- OpenAI's new "education for countries" program cites projections that nearly 40% of core worker skills will change by 2030; the first partner cohort is Estonia, Greece, Italy, Jordan, Kazakhstan, Slovakia, Trinidad and Tobago, and the UAE.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- Google, with the Princeton Review, is serving free full-length practice SATs through Gemini with instant feedback, and awarded $500,000 to Cal State Fullerton for AI-literacy training for educators.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- At Davos, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella warned AI "will quickly lose even the social permission" to consume scarce energy unless it delivers clear benefits, compared the moment to the PC's adoption ("4 billion people typing"), and invoked Jevons paradox — cheaper tokens mean more demand, not a crash.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- JPMorgan CEO Jamie Dimon said AI "will eliminate jobs" and warned of civil unrest if transitions aren't phased — citing 2 million American truck drivers who could fall from $150,000 salaries to $25,000 jobs — urging governments and business to collaborate on retraining.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang argued labor shortages, not layoffs, are the real issue — "the largest infrastructure buildout in human history" creating trades jobs for plumbers, electricians, and steel workers; Mike Rowe of Dirty Jobs endorsed the message while noting the workforce is "nowhere near ready."[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]

# Takeaways

Whittemore reads all these headlines as one larger story: society's adaptation to AI. He welcomes the data-center commitments while insisting they should have come sooner and should aim higher than avoiding harm, and he flags the open question of whether long-term power contracts can bring consumer relief fast enough to matter politically by November, given the long lead times for new power plants.[^more-ai-datacenter-community-commitments-nfchOeBJxtc]

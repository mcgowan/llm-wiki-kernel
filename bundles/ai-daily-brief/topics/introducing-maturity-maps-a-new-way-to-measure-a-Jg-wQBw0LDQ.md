# Overview

On day two of his "build week," Nathaniel Whittemore introduces AI maturity maps, a framework developed at AI Daily Brief and his enterprise AI strategy company Superintelligent for measuring where organizations actually stand on AI and agent readiness. His premise: everyone is adapting to AI "without a map," and legacy benchmarks like Gartner's Magic Quadrant "have literally never been less useful," because success no longer hinges on vendor selection. Without peer benchmarks, a company growing content output 30% year over year can't know its competitors grew 50% — so better data is a practical necessity, not a vanity exercise.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]

Maturity maps score six dimensions — deployment depth, systems integration, data, outcomes, people, and governance — across ten knowledge-work functions on a five-point scale, where three is a subjective "on-track" line and the average organization sits mostly below it, effectively visualizing the capability overhang. The Q2 maps draw on more than 480 studies and surveys from the quarter, with a combined respondent base over 150,000 professionals in 50-plus countries, aggregated by Whittemore's always-on research agents. The headline findings: high claimed adoption but shallow embedding, a persistent gap between leader and worker perceptions, chronic underinvestment in people, and data access as the floor constraint capping everything else.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]

# Key points

- The six maturity dimensions are deployment depth (assistants vs. workflow automations vs. autonomous agentic systems), systems integration, data, outcomes, people (skills plus attitudes), and governance, mapped across ten functions: customer service, engineering, IT, sales, marketing, HR, operations, finance, legal, and product.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Scores run one (significantly behind) to five (significantly ahead), with three as the "on-track" line — a subjective judgment of where an average organization should be; in the vast majority of cases the average organization sits behind it.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Inputs include AIDB Intel research, Superintelligent's thousands of monthly voice-agent interviews, and an agentic aggregation system: 480+ studies from the quarter, 150,000+ combined survey respondents across 50+ countries, spanning Big Four consulting research, earnings statements, analyst firms (Gartner, Forrester, IDC), annual function surveys, academic and government research, and behavioral datasets like Jellyfish's benchmark of 200,000 engineers across 700 companies and 20 million PRs.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Dominant Q2 pattern: an "adoption embedding gap" — every function-specific survey shows high claimed adoption but low depth and utilization, an applied capability overhang inside organizations.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- People is the most neglected pillar: seven of ten functions scored one (significantly behind); Deloitte found 93% of AI spend goes to infrastructure and only 7% to anything people-related; in customer service, 72% of leaders call AI training adequate while 55% of employees disagree.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Data is "the floor constraint that caps all the others": eight of ten functions scored one or 1.5, since without proprietary context (code base, customer history, deal data) organizations can't get past basic assisted usage.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Outcome measurement is universally weak because adoption pressure left no one time to build ROI measurement first; Whittemore predicts ROI measurement will be the year's biggest "glow-up."[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Only customer service (deployment depth, systems), engineering and IT (deployment depth, systems, people), and finance (governance) hit on-track anywhere; 69% of CFOs report advanced AI risk governance thanks to SOX compliance and regulatory muscle memory, while finance is significantly behind on everything else — "they know how to control AI, but haven't figured out how to use it."[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- Sales is "the cleanest example of the adoption mirage": 88% of sales teams say they use AI but only 24% have it in revenue workflows — much adoption is reps using ChatGPT in a separate browser tab; the autonomous SDR dream has not come to fruition.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- IT governance numbers are alarming for the function that owns governance: only 54% have centralized frameworks, 50% of AI agents are unmonitored, and 88% have had security incidents; customer service shows stress warning signs (87% of CS workers report high stress) as AI absorbs routine cases and leaves humans the harder emotional ones.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]
- A companion tool, an 18-question quiz at besuper.ai/quiz, lets organizations see where they stand versus the on-track line and the average; a related "AI opportunity radar" organizes use cases as prime time, emerging, or frontier.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]

# Takeaways

Whittemore frames maturity maps as nascent — this is their first full quarter — and flags the biggest limitation himself: a single on-track line can't reasonably judge a 10-person startup and a 10,000-person enterprise alike, so future versions should add gradations by organization size and industry. He floats a "tortoise and hare" question for finance: when the well-governed-but-underdeployed function finally figures out deployment, will it leapfrog functions that deployed first and governed later? His closing argument is that in this moment "we need all the data that we can get," and the more organizations share information and build common benchmarks for what on-track means, the better off everyone will be.[^introducing-maturity-maps-a-new-way-to-measure-a-Jg-wQBw0LDQ]

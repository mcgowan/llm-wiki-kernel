# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore uses a lull in bubble talk to dispassionately size the AI economy, walking through Exponential View's "State of the AI Economy" report — a deduplicated, confidence-scored analysis of over a thousand AI companies. The headline: AI companies banked $110B over the past 12 months and sit at a $175B annualized run rate, growing three times faster than any prior IT wave; the industry now adds a billion dollars of cumulative revenue in under two days, versus 180 days in 2023. The report argues the largest buildout in tech history is "paying back for now" — quarterly revenue began exceeding capex depreciation in Q4 of last year, and older GPUs are earning yields into years 7-9, well past their 6-year depreciation life.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]

The headlines cover leaked code suggesting Anthropic's Fable relaunch will be credit-based and require identity verification, Senator Mark Warner's discussion-draft bill on consumer AI agents (agent access rights and a "duty of loyalty"), California's statewide half-price Claude deal, Amazon losing its wholesale-rate Claude pricing, Meta restricting Codex and Claude Code in its Applied AI division to avoid the "distillation trap," Google capping Meta's Gemini usage amid the compute crunch, AWS hiking GPU capacity-block prices 20%, and memory-price "RAMageddon" with Micron targeting 84% gross margins.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]

# Key points

- Leaked Claude app code strings suggest Fable's relaunch will bill usage separately on credits and require identity documents ("Your credits will be added once your identity is verified"); Whittemore predicts nearly everyone will comply despite the outcry, noting Dario Amodei himself invoked the gun-license comparison for Mythos.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Senator Mark Warner's 25-page discussion draft would protect third-party agent access (e.g., your own Open Claw shopping on Amazon) and impose a "duty of loyalty" barring undisclosed-partnership steering; it covers only consumer-facing agents and is unlikely to move this year.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Governor Newsom announced the first statewide AI rollout: all California departments and local governments get Claude at 50% off with free workforce training.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Anthropic renegotiated Amazon's sweetheart compute-hour pricing to token-based rates starting next year; Amazon is reportedly exploring OpenAI or in-house Nova models, and some engineers have been proactively distilling Anthropic models.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Meta's Applied AI division must solve coding problems without Codex or Claude Code to avoid contaminating training data for its own frontier coding model — the "distillation trap," since distillation violates both labs' terms of service.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Google capped Meta's Gemini usage in March amid the compute crunch; AWS raised EC2 capacity-block prices 20% for Nvidia GPUs (Trainium exempt); SemiAnalysis notes falling spot H100 prices (down 40% from May peak) alongside rising contract prices signal committed production demand, not weakness.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- RAMageddon: Apple raised product prices up to 15%, Micron quadrupled prices in a year and targets 84% gross margins (third-highest US profit margin behind Google and Nvidia), Apple petitioned to buy memory from blacklisted Chinese supplier CXMT, and a class action accuses Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron of running a cartel.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Exponential View headline numbers: $110B banked in 12 months, $175B annualized run rate, 3x faster growth than any prior IT wave; the 2026 global semiconductor market is projected at $1.5T, nearly doubling from $792B.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- The power sector reawakened: US electricity generation was flat 2008-2024 but is now growing at 9 TWh/month annually — 150% of the 1950-2008 historical average.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Hyperscaler and neocloud capex will hit $848B this year ($2T cumulative since 2020); revenues cover ongoing expense but not yet the cumulative bill; AI revenue equals just 0.42% of US GDP versus IT's 9.4%, up 3x from Q1 2025.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Token economics: agentic coding tasks use ~1,200x the tokens of chat tasks; global volume exceeds 30 quadrillion tokens/month, growing 14x year over year; blended price per million tokens fell from $17 to $2 (mid-2024 to mid-2026) while capability rose, and energy monetization per gigawatt roughly doubled.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]
- Business validation: 33% of public companies now claim AI impact on earnings calls (20% quantified); companies in the top quartile of AI spend intensity grew revenue 100%+ over three years versus 15-20% for no-AI-spend companies — a 92% growth differential.[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's summary: the AI economy is on a $175B run rate, growing three times faster than previous platform shifts, driving a compute supercycle and energy buildout, with chips earning economic value beyond their expected lifespan. The investment case reduces to whether falling prices move enough token volume to earn a return on capex — and the indicators are more positive than the average discourse suggests. None of this precludes market over-exuberance, but he closes with what he calls the most insightful AI-bubble tweet ever, from OpenAI's Roon: "Not enough people are emotionally prepared for if it's not a bubble."[^how-big-is-the-ai-economy-gP8A3x2mFRM]

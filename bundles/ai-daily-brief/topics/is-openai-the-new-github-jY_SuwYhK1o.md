# Overview

In this headlines edition, Nathaniel Whittemore leads with The Information's report that OpenAI is developing an internal alternative to GitHub, spurred by a surge in outages on Microsoft's code platform — 37 in February alone versus an average of 17 per month the previous year. The move echoes Mitchell Hashimoto's December quip that "the AI companies are on track to become GitHub faster than GitHub is becoming an AI company." While some frame it as OpenAI-Microsoft rift escalation or SaaS-pocalypse behavior, Whittemore reads it as the start of an inevitable category shift given how much code now flows through the AI companies — with the real prize, per Replit's Amjad Masad, being the layer that understands how code connects across services and teams, where agents actually need to operate.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]

The rest of the episode rounds up Meta's new applied AI engineering organization, Amazon's exploration of ad tech for chatbots and agents, US officials weighing caps on Nvidia chip sales to China, Apple's M5 device launch, and — what Whittemore calls "sneakily powerful" — Stripe's new feature for automatically billing AI token usage, which he sees as a major step toward viable usage-based pricing for AI apps.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]

# Key points

- OpenAI's GitHub alternative is in early stages, months from completion, and intended for internal use first — though, Whittemore notes, "so was Claude Code"; former GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke left Microsoft earlier in the year to build an agentic-era GitHub rebuild with agentic code review closing the loop on autonomous code generation.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Microsoft attributed GitHub's outages to human error and Azure problems during a multi-year migration off GitHub's proprietary servers.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Meta formed a new applied AI engineering org bridging Reality Labs and the Meta Superintelligence Lab: two flat teams of 50 (interfaces/internal tooling and data collection/refinement) under a single manager, building the "data engine" — mirroring TBD Labs under AI CEO Alexandr Wang and Zuckerberg's view that AI lets "a single very talented person" do what once took big teams.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Amazon is discussing technology to place ads in chatbots and agents with major websites including Pinterest; Amazon's ad business generated $68.6 billion last year, its fastest-growing division at 22% growth, positioning it for a land grab over the AI-ad clearinghouse.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Bloomberg reports US trade officials are considering a 75,000-chip-per-customer cap on Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325 sales into China, within a previously unreported 1-million-unit total limit — less than half what Alibaba, Tencent, and ByteDance each sought (~200,000 chips), capping Chinese data centers at roughly 100 megawatts versus Western multi-gigawatt clusters and xAI's Colossus, now reportedly at 550,000 GPUs.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Chips are on the agenda for the upcoming Trump-Xi meeting, though the Iran war and larger geopolitics could overshadow the trade talks.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Apple unveiled M5-powered MacBook Air and MacBook Pro models with a new "neural accelerator" component, marketed squarely at AI use cases.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]
- Stripe previewed automatic usage-fee billing for AI apps — e.g., a 30% markup on API calls tracked and billed by Stripe, integrating with Vercel and OpenRouter — replacing back-end token tracking and lump-sum bills; Whittemore recalls Replit briefly running at -14% gross margins when token demand surged under flat-rate pricing.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's two editorial notes: on the GitHub story, he doubts the rift or SaaS-pocalypse framings and sees a natural, inevitable shift in who owns the code layer of the agentic era. On Stripe, he calls the billing feature "a massive, massive step" toward usage-based pricing becoming a viable business model — tokens can now be priced as a commodity all the way to the end user, and even if some users pay more for what they consume, he argues the result will be a much healthier and more sustainable ecosystem.[^is-openai-the-new-github-jY_SuwYhK1o]

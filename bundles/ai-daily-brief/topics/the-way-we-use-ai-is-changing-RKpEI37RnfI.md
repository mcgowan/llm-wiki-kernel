# Overview

The headlines cover an unusual convergence in AI policy, with Donald Trump confirming the government is exploring equity stakes in major AI labs and finding common ground with Bernie Sanders' proposal to tax AI company equity into a sovereign wealth fund, while OpenAI reportedly pitches donating equity to seed a public fund. Whittemore also covers Google's $920 million-per-month compute rental from SpaceX and Nvidia's multi-year memory deal with SK Hynix.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]

The main episode argues that most commentary is misreading the Financial Times report on OpenAI's ChatGPT super-app overhaul. Whittemore concedes the IPO and revenue motives are real but calls it reductive to treat them as primary. His reading: a widening advantage gap has opened between agent users and chat users, an even further gap has opened between agent users and the loop-designing vanguard, and changing the interface is how the labs can pull everyone forward.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]

- Nvidia signed a two-year deal with SK Hynix securing high-bandwidth memory for the Vera Rubin ramp and joining as a design partner on memory chips for physical AI, personal AI, and infrastructure; Jensen Huang said the whole supply chain "from wafers to silicon photonics to cable connectors" is in shortage.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]
- The Financial Times reported OpenAI's biggest ChatGPT overhaul since launch, turning it into a super app combining coding tools and agents, rolling out in coming weeks and steering users toward coding, image generation, and partner apps, with ChatGPT positioned as a gateway to higher-value products.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]
- Cited figures: nearly a billion ChatGPT users with about 50 million paying; roughly 2 million business customers at 40% of revenue, targeting 50% by year end; OpenAI CFO Sarah Friar said free users average about seven turns a day, the first paid tier about 15, Plus about 3x free, and Pro about 11x.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]
- Whittemore dates the inflection to roughly November 2025 through January 2026, when agents became viable and coding tools proved useful to all knowledge workers, sending the power-user advantage gap "into overdrive" — compounding value for agent users versus linear gains for chat users.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]
- On the frontier of practice: Peter Steinberger says you should be designing loops that prompt your agents rather than prompting agents; Claude Code creator Boris Cherney described moving from running five to ten Claudes in parallel to writing loops that prompt Claude, calling loop-writing his job now. - Evidence of a teaching gap: a post asking what loops mean for non-coders drew over 300,000 views, Cloudflare CTO Dane Knecht's question about loop usage drew about 250 replies, and Emad Mostaque noted loops magnify error rates on non-coding analytical problems.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]
- On coding agent preference, a Ben Holmes poll of nearly 2,100 votes put the Codex app at 51.1% and terminal CLIs at 30.9%.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's answer to whether the overhaul is about money and the IPO is "yes, but," and he notes how often the investor class assumes every corporate action is aimed primarily at impressing them. He argues the deeper driver is that the most valuable AI use cases are simply not chat, and that with agent users compounding value while chat users gain linearly — and a further vanguard already past prompting into loop design — interface changes are the labs' most direct lever for democratizing those experiences. He acknowledges the token economics reinforce this, since loop-runners burn far more tokens, and ties it to his recurring theme of the shift from the token subsidy era to the token scarcity era. But he insists the motivation is to get more people experiencing agents at scale, not just to move the revenue scoreboard, and notes that with almost nobody taught how to work this way, learning materials plus redesigned interfaces are the two available paths.[^the-way-we-use-ai-is-changing-RKpEI37RnfI]

# Overview

For the final episode of 2025, Nathaniel Whittemore lays out a "10 weekend AI resolution" — a self-guided path to AI fluency built from ten modular weekend projects, each with a concrete deliverable, a beginner/intermediate default, an advanced modifier, and an expected couple of hours of work. The projects are practical by default ("forcing outputs, not theory"), completable, skippable in any order, but compounding if done in sequence; he suggests scoring each on outcome quality, time saved, repeatability, and real-world reuse, and offers a community hub at aidbnewyear.com — a free experience with no paid upsell.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]

Whittemore recommends about 30 minutes of prep — a project folder plus picking one automation platform (Lindy, n8n, or Make) and one vibe-coding platform (Replit, Lovable, or Google AI Studio) — so each weekend is about doing the work, not setting up accounts. He notes he built the episode's own materials with Claude and Genspark, practicing what he preaches.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]

# Key points

- Weekend 1 — vibe-code a resolution tracker: a deployable web app with checkboxes, notes, and a progress bar for the ten weeks; advanced version adds authentication, collaboration mode, or mobile optimization.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 2 — model mapping: run the same tasks (deep research, writing, strategy, data analysis, visualization) through multiple models to build personal instincts, ending in a one-page rule-of-thumb document; most people leave value on the table by defaulting to one model.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 3 — deep research sprint: pick a real decision (competitor analysis, pricing, product research), iterate hard, ask for disconfirming evidence — closing the gap between "AI can theoretically do research" and "I trust this enough to make a decision." He cites studies showing shockingly low usage of deep research features.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 4 — data analysis: clean a real dataset (bank statements, Spotify history, or Kaggle), calculate 5–10 metrics, test three hypotheses, and write a one-page insights memo; advanced version builds a repeatable monthly pipeline.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 5 — visual reasoning with Nano Banana Pro or ChatGPT Images 1.5: reason through visualization trade-offs, generate alternate designs, and apply visual QA; done when the visual explains the idea faster than words.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 6 — information pipeline: use NotebookLM and Gamma — "extremely powerful" but underutilized tools — to turn raw inputs into executive summaries, FAQs, decks, podcasts, and even websites via one reusable workflow.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekends 7 and 8 — two automations forming a "minimum viable automation stack": a content distribution machine, then a productivity automation (inbox follow-up system, lead response system, or meeting prep bot), each with trigger, transformation, routing, human approval, and logging.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 9 — context engineering: build a professional context document and personal "AI operating system" (playbook, prompt library, capture inbox, 15-minute weekly review), since 2026 will be about giving AI better context — though he cautions that LLM memory pushes could change this fast.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Weekend 10 — build something that uses AI itself via Google AI Studio: a knowledge-trained chatbot, a voice agent (language partner, mock interviewer), or a mini document-processing agent; advanced: ship it to real users and iterate.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]
- Bonus weekend — agent evaluation gauntlet: run standardized research, operations, and production tasks through Manus and Genspark versus base LLMs, scoring accuracy, traceability, constraint-following, edit-free usefulness, and repeatability to learn what to delegate to agents.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]

# Takeaways

Whittemore's pitch is that completing the ten weekends yields a personal tracker, a deep research workflow, visual reasoning skills, two active automations, a deployed AI-powered tool, a model topography, an analysis pipeline, an info-processing stack, and a personal AI operating system — putting the finisher "ahead of 99.99% of people when it comes to the full breadth of what AI can do." The deeper goal is habits and workflows still in use months later, with the caveat that in AI there's no guarantee what we'll be doing in six months resembles today.[^ai-new-year-s-the-10-week-ai-resolution-EhQeGgZxdHU]

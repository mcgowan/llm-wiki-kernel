# Overview

After roughly 19 days offline, Fable 5 returned: Anthropic announced Tuesday night at 7:52 p.m. that the Department of Commerce had lifted export controls, with access restored globally to all paid subscribers from July 1. Nathaniel Whittemore recounts the resolution — Anthropic's testing showed many less-capable models (Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, Kimi K2.7, even Haiku 4.5) could reproduce the Amazon-reported jailbreak findings, and the "reported technique... only involved routine defensive cybersecurity work" — plus the new 99%-success classifier targeting the reported behavior, validated by Commerce's Center for AI Standards and Innovation as "extraordinarily strong," at the cost of more false positives on benign coding tasks.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]

The headlines cover the zeitgeist quest to cut inference costs — The Information's report that OpenAI found a technique halving inference requirements, serving all signed-out ChatGPT users on just 100 GPUs — plus Base44's custom Base 1 model, AWS's $1 billion forward-deployed-engineer unit, Claude "tag" coming to Microsoft Teams, and SpaceX discounting Starlink in Memphis to quell Colossus data-center backlash. The main episode closes with Whittemore's contrarian guidance on what to try during the one-week subsidized window before Fable moves to usage credits.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]

# Key points

- Fable 5 returned for all global users with no rumored KYC/citizenship regime; it's included for up to 50% of weekly usage limits until July 7, after which access requires usage credits. Mythos restrictions remain, with approved US firms retaining access and Glasswing expansion under discussion.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick framed the resolution as unprecedented public-private cooperation; Dean Ball welcomed progress but warned "we have no idea what Anthropic did to make the model safe" and that opacity "will not lend itself well to a stable, investable, trustworthy industry" — noting GPT-5.6 is in the government's licensing queue.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- Panic over "some routine tasks like coding and debugging will fall back to Opus 4.8" was walked back by the Claude Code team: only a small fraction of routine coding tasks get flagged.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- Claude Sonnet 5 launched the same day — Anthropic's "most agentic Sonnet yet," a few points shy of Opus 4.8 on SWE-bench Pro and Terminal Bench 2.1 but slightly ahead of it on GDPval; intro API pricing of $2/$10 per million tokens until end of August (then $3/$15, versus Opus's $5/$25).[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- Artificial Analysis found Sonnet 5 used ~40% more output tokens per task than Sonnet 4.6 and about 3x the agentic turns, making full-bench runs more expensive than Opus 4.8 and even Fable at max effort — prompting "dead on arrival" takes, countered by users like Ben Davis who found it excellent when used differently ("basically an automatic Ralph loop... spawning tons of sub-agents"); some see it as the implementer to Fable 5's advisor.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- OpenAI's undisclosed inference optimization cut requirements in half in tests on signed-out free users; Whittemore cautions it may involve quality tradeoffs and cites Benchmark's "AI mom test" — most casual usage doesn't need frontier models. Harry Stebbings reported five founders claiming 75%+ inference-spend cuts with no performance change.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- Base44 launched Base 1, a fine-tuned open-source model trained on hundreds of millions of platform interactions, following Cursor's Composer playbook: narrow models good enough for web-app building, cost control, and harness-model co-development.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- AWS is investing $1 billion in a forward-deployed-engineer division focused on healthcare, government, and financial services, following OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft/EY moves; the AI Engineer World's Fair even hosted an AI FDE mini-conference.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]
- Anthropic plans to bring Claude tag — an organization-centric agent with persistent memory, effectively Claude Code summoned into a channel — to Microsoft Teams, raising platform power-struggle questions after Satya Nadella claimed Claude plugins reinforce Microsoft's "structural position in knowledge work."[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]

# Takeaways

Whittemore agrees Fable 5 excels at your hardest technical problems, but pushes back on the consensus that it's wasted on routine, non-technical work. In his experience it "blew GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 out of the water" on strategic thinking — uniquely accepting parts of pushback while sticking to its guns on others, unlike steerable models that cave immediately — and it's token-light for that use. On writing, contra Every's "sharp judgment trapped in familiar prose" vibe check, he found it far better at instruction-following and rubric-matching, with fewer AI-isms, even if blank-page writing isn't obviously improved. His bottom line: everyone has strategic questions in flight, so experiment with Fable on them during the subsidized week — and he wouldn't blame anyone for spending July 4th inside hacking on a big project.[^fable-is-back-here-s-what-you-should-try-first-ztCLWKwyMJY]

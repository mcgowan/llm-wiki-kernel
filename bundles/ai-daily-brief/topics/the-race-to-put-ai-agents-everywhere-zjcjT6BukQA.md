# Overview

Nathaniel Whittemore surveys what he calls the race to productize agents and make them enterprise-ready as Q1 2026 closes. He frames Open Claw as the key story of the quarter — the instantiation of the capability shift he calls "AI's second moment" — and describes a "clawfication of the world": a wave of simplifying forks (Nanobot, Zero Claw, Pico Claw, Nano Claw), security-focused self-hosted variants (Open Fang, Hermes, Multus, Iron Claw), and integrated agent products from companies like Notion and Perplexity.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]

The immediate catalyst is Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's GTC speech declaring that "every software company in the world needs to have an Open Claw strategy," alongside the launch of Nemo Claw, a security toolkit built on the Open Claw project. Whittemore pairs this with a cluster of same-day announcements — Manis's "My Computer" desktop app, Adaptive Computer, and OpenAI's reported refocus on enterprise and coding — to argue that Q2 will be an "absolute sprint" to get agents diffused across the business world.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]

# Key points

- Jensen Huang said at Nvidia's GTC event that "every software company in the world needs to have an Open Claw strategy," and compared Open Claw's timing to Linux, Kubernetes, and HTML.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Nvidia's Nemo Claw is a software toolkit built on Open Claw that adds an isolated sandbox with policy-based access control and guardrails, model- and hardware-agnostic, supporting cloud or local models; Open Claw creator Peter Steinberger collaborated on it.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Kevin Simback of Delphi Labs argued Open Claw "opened the door," proving that "people don't want AI chat, they want to get work done" and that broad machine access is "both insanely useful and mildly terrifying."[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Notion introduced custom agents at the end of February; Perplexity released Computer, Computer for Enterprise (operating from Slack with claimed connections to 400+ applications), and Personal Computer, an always-on local Mac mini offering; CEO Arvind Srinivas argued "the AI is the computer."[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Manis (purchased by Meta in December) announced a desktop app with a "My Computer" feature bringing its cloud-sandbox agent onto local machines, including building fully working Mac apps.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Adaptive launched Adaptive Computer, an always-on personal computer for agents, pitching "encoded memory" that learns how tools like Square work; its example use case was a hardware store owner adding 47 products from a spreadsheet.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- The Wall Street Journal reported OpenAI is "done with side quests," with applications CEO Fidji Simo telling staff to nail business productivity and that "we are very much acting as if it's a code red."[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- OpenAI shipped native sub-agent integration into Codex; Greg Brockman said GPT-5.4 ramped faster than any prior API model — 5 trillion tokens per day within a week and a $1B annualized net-new-revenue run rate.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
- Whittemore's own programs show demand: more than 7,000 people signed up for Claw Camp, and Enterprise Claw participants split roughly half-and-half between Open Claw and generic agent tools like Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor.[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]

# Takeaways

Whittemore concludes that if Q1 2026 was the realization that agents are here — mass experimentation with the form factors Open Claw introduced — then Q2 is set up as a sprint to productize those agents for broad diffusion, especially in the enterprise. The open question he flags is whether old productization wisdom about simplifying for wider audiences still holds when the breakout product was an incredibly complex system; he suspects a spectrum of complexity bands for different users, and predicts "just about everything that can be tried will be tried in the quarter to come."[^the-race-to-put-ai-agents-everywhere-zjcjT6BukQA]
